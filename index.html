Company Information

Transparency, Trust, and Excellence

🏢

About Our Company

Established over 15 years ago, Honest Travel Tours and Expedition is a fully licensed and registered travel company in Nepal. We are authorized by the Nepal Tourism Board and certified by the Nepal Mountaineering Association.

🤝

Our Responsibility

We believe in responsible tourism that benefits local communities and preserves natural environments. We employ local guides and porters at fair wages, support village schools and health posts, and contribute to conservation projects.

💰

Our Pricing Philosophy

We believe in transparent, fair pricing with no hidden costs. Our quotes include all mentioned services, permits, taxes, and fees. We work on reasonable margins that allow us to pay our staff fairly while offering you the best value.

👨‍👨‍👦‍👦

Group Sizes

We maintain small group sizes to ensure personalized attention and minimal environmental impact. Our typical group size is 6-12 people for scheduled departures. We also specialize in private trips for couples, families, and custom groups.

👨‍💼

Our Professional Staff

Our guides are government-licensed, wilderness first-aid certified, and fluent in multiple languages. Many have summited major Himalayan peaks. Our support staff—porters, cooks, drivers—are trained, insured, and equipped with proper gear.

🙋

Our Clients, Our Selves

We treat every client as we would want to be treated—with respect, honesty, and genuine care. Your safety is our priority, your comfort is our concern, and your satisfaction is our success. Many of our clients return for multiple trips and refer friends and family.

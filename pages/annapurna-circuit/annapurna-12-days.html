Annapurna Circuit Trek - 12 Days Himalayan Adventure

12 Days - Classic Trekking Route of Nepal

Classic Himalayan Trekking Experience

The Annapurna Circuit Trek represents one of Nepal's most celebrated trekking routes, offering a comprehensive Himalayan experience across diverse landscapes. This 12-day itinerary has been carefully designed by our experienced team at Honest Travel Tours to provide an optimal balance of adventure, cultural immersion, and safety. While traditional routes may require 12-13 days, our refined schedule maintains the essence of the journey while being accessible to trekkers with limited time.

Key Advantages of Our 12-Day Itinerary

  • Optimized Route: Strategic use of road transport from Besisahar to Manang reduces walking time without compromising the experience
  • Altitude Management: Carefully paced ascent with proper acclimatization days to minimize altitude sickness risk
  • Cultural Diversity: Experience the transition from Hindu lowlands to Buddhist high mountain communities
  • Scenic Variety: From subtropical forests to arid high-altitude landscapes resembling the Tibetan plateau
  • Logistical Excellence: All accommodations, meals, and transportation pre-arranged for a seamless experience
  • Expert Guidance: Licensed trekking guides with extensive knowledge of the region and emergency training

Why Choose Our Annapurna Circuit Trek

Our 12-day Annapurna Circuit itinerary represents years of field experience and refinement. By incorporating strategic road transfers where appropriate, we've created a trek that captures all the highlights while being more accessible to modern trekkers. You'll cross the legendary Thorong La Pass, explore traditional villages, witness breathtaking mountain vistas, and experience Nepal's renowned hospitality—all within a well-paced schedule that respects your time and physical capabilities.

Detailed Itinerary

DAY 1 ARRIVAL IN KATHMANDU 1,440m

Welcome to Nepal: Arrive at Tribhuvan International Airport where our representative will greet you with a traditional welcome. Transfer to your hotel in the vibrant Thamel district. After check-in, attend a comprehensive trek briefing with your guide who will review the itinerary, check your equipment, and answer any questions. The evening is free to explore Thamel's bustling streets, restaurants, and shops. Enjoy your first taste of Nepali cuisine at a local restaurant.

Meals: Welcome Dinner Included

Accommodation: Hotel in Kathmandu

DAY 2 DRIVE TO BESISAHAR 760m

Journey Begins: Early morning departure from Kathmandu after breakfast. The scenic drive (approximately 7-8 hours) follows the winding highway alongside the Trishuli and Marsyangdi rivers. Witness the landscape transform from urban to rural, with terraced fields and traditional villages dotting the hillsides. Stop for lunch at a roadside restaurant with mountain views. Arrive in Besisahar, the district headquarters of Lamjung and gateway to the Annapurna region. Check into your lodge, explore the small town, and prepare for tomorrow's adventure.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, and Dinner Included

Accommodation: Lodge in Besisahar

Drive Duration: 7-8 hours

DAY 3 DRIVE TO CHAME 2,670m

Entering the Mountains: After breakfast, board 4WD vehicles for the adventurous drive to Chame (approximately 5 hours). The rough mountain road winds through lush forests and alongside the raging Marsyangdi River. Pass through several traditional villages, noticing the gradual change in architecture and culture. Enjoy spectacular views of Manaslu (8,163m) and other peaks. Arrive in Chame, the administrative headquarters of the Manang district, by early afternoon. The remainder of the day is free to explore this picturesque town surrounded by pine forests.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, and Dinner Included

Accommodation: Tea House in Chame

Drive Duration: 5 hours

DAY 4 TREK TO PISANG 3,230m

First Trekking Day: Begin your actual trekking today with a 5-hour walk to Pisang. The trail follows the Marsyangdi River through beautiful pine forests. Pass through the villages of Bhratang and Dhukur Pokhari, where you'll notice the distinctive Tibetan Buddhist influence increasing. The trail offers stunning views of Annapurna II and IV. Arrive in Pisang, divided into Upper and Lower sections. Upper Pisang offers magnificent views of the Annapurna range and the iconic Pisang Peak. Overnight in a traditional tea house.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, and Dinner Included

Accommodation: Tea House in Pisang

Trek Duration: 5 hours

DAY 5 TREK TO MANANG 3,450m

Heart of Manang: Today's 5-hour trek takes you to the culturally rich village of Manang. Choose between the upper route via Ghyaru (more challenging with better views) or the lower route through Hongde (easier). The upper route offers breathtaking panoramas of Annapurna III and IV, Gangapurna, and Tilicho Peak. Manang is a major settlement in the valley, with distinct Tibetan culture, ancient monasteries, and stunning mountain backdrop. Upon arrival, explore the village, visit the century-old Barge Monastery, and acclimatize to the increasing altitude.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, and Dinner Included

Accommodation: Tea House in Manang

Trek Duration: 5 hours

DAY 6 ACCLIMATIZATION IN MANANG

Altitude Adjustment: This crucial rest day allows your body to adapt to the higher altitude, significantly reducing the risk of altitude sickness. After breakfast, embark on an acclimatization hike to either Gangapurna Lake (3,600m) or the viewpoint at Praken Gompa (3,900m). Both options offer spectacular views and gentle exercise to aid adaptation. In the afternoon, explore Manang village, visit the Himalayan Rescue Association clinic for an altitude talk, or simply relax with a book. Early to bed in preparation for higher altitudes tomorrow.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, and Dinner Included

Accommodation: Tea House in Manang

Activity: Acclimatization hike (2-3 hours)

DAY 7 TREK TO YAK KHARKA 4,110m

Higher Elevations: A shorter but important ascent day as you climb to Yak Kharka (meaning "yak pasture"). The 4-hour trek follows the Jarsang Khola valley, with the landscape becoming more arid and alpine. Notice the vegetation thinning as you gain altitude. The trail offers excellent views of the surrounding peaks, including Chulu East and West. Arrive at the small settlement of Yak Kharka by early afternoon, allowing plenty of time to rest and acclimatize. This gradual ascent is key to successful adaptation for crossing Thorong La Pass.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, and Dinner Included

Accommodation: Tea House in Yak Kharka

Trek Duration: 4 hours

DAY 8 TREK TO THORONG HIGH CAMP 4,600m

Approaching the Pass: Another short but steep 4-hour climb to Thorong High Camp, your base for tomorrow's pass crossing. The trail ascends steadily through increasingly rugged terrain. Pass through Thorong Phedi (4,450m), where many trekkers traditionally stayed before the high camp was built. Continue ascending to High Camp, arriving by early afternoon. Use the remainder of the day to prepare for tomorrow's challenging day: organize your daypack, charge devices if possible, hydrate well, and get an early night. The temperature drops significantly at this altitude.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, and Dinner Included

Accommodation: Basic Lodge at High Camp

Trek Duration: 4 hours

DAY 9 CROSS THORONG LA PASS TO MUKTINATH 5,416m (Pass) / 3,800m

The Challenge Day: An early start (around 4-5 AM) is essential for safe pass crossing. The 7-hour trek begins with a steep 3-4 hour climb to Thorong La Pass (5,416m), the highest point of the trek. Celebrate your achievement at the pass with prayer flags fluttering in the wind and breathtaking 360-degree Himalayan views. After photos and celebrations, begin the long descent to Muktinath (3,800m) - over 1,600m down! The descent is challenging but rewards with changing landscapes as you enter the Mustang region. Arrive in Muktinath exhausted but exhilarated.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, and Dinner Included

Accommodation: Lodge in Muktinath

Trek Duration: 7-8 hours

DAY 10 DRIVE TO POKHARA 800m

Return to Civilization: After breakfast, visit the sacred Muktinath Temple complex, important to both Hindus and Buddhists. Then board a local deluxe bus for the scenic 10-hour drive to Pokhara. The journey takes you through the dramatic Kali Gandaki Gorge (world's deepest gorge), past Tatopani's hot springs, and along the banks of the Kali Gandaki River. Notice the dramatic change in climate and vegetation as you descend. Arrive in beautiful Pokhara by evening, check into your hotel by Phewa Lake, and enjoy a well-deserved hot shower and celebration dinner.

Meals: Breakfast and Dinner Included

Accommodation: Hotel in Pokhara

Drive Duration: 10 hours

DAY 11 RETURN TO KATHMANDU 1,400m

Options Available: Choose between a scenic 6-7 hour drive or a 25-minute flight back to Kathmandu (flight cost additional). The drive offers beautiful views of hills, rivers, and rural life, with lunch en route. The flight provides spectacular aerial views of the Himalayas. Arrive in Kathmandu by afternoon and transfer to your hotel. The remainder of the day is free for relaxation, shopping for souvenirs, or exploring areas you missed earlier. In the evening, enjoy a farewell dinner with your trekking team to celebrate your successful Annapurna Circuit completion.

Meals: Breakfast and Farewell Dinner Included

Accommodation: Hotel in Kathmandu

Travel Duration: 6-7 hours drive or 25-minute flight

DAY 12 DEPARTURE FROM NEPAL

Journey Completion: After breakfast, check out from your hotel (standard check-out time is 12 PM). Our representative will assist with airport transfers according to your flight schedule. Depart with incredible memories of Himalayan vistas, cultural encounters, personal achievements, and new friendships. Carry with you the sense of accomplishment from completing one of the world's great treks and the hope to return to Nepal's majestic mountains.

Meals: Breakfast Included

Until we meet again in the Himalayas! Safe travels!

Tour Concludes.

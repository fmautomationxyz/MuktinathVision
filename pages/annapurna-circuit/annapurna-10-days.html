10-Day Detailed Itinerary

DAY 1 ARRIVAL IN KATHMANDU 1,440m

Welcome to Nepal: Arrive at Tribhuvan International Airport where our representative will greet you. Transfer to your hotel in Thamel. After check-in, attend a comprehensive trek briefing with your guide who will review the itinerary, check your equipment, and answer questions. Evening free to explore Thamel's bustling streets.

Meals: Welcome Dinner Included

Accommodation: Hotel in Kathmandu

DAY 2 DRIVE KATHMANDU TO BESISAHAR 760m

Scenic Drive: Early morning departure from Kathmandu after breakfast. Drive approximately 7-8 hours following the Trishuli and Marsyangdi rivers. Witness landscape transformation from urban to rural. Arrive in Besisahar, gateway to the Annapurna region. Check into lodge and prepare for adventure.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, and Dinner Included

Accommodation: Lodge in Besisahar

Drive Duration: 7-8 hours

DAY 3 DRIVE TO CHAME 2,670m

Mountain Entry: After breakfast, board 4WD vehicles for adventurous drive to Chame (5 hours). Rough mountain road winds through lush forests alongside Marsyangdi River. Pass traditional villages with changing architecture. Enjoy views of Manaslu (8,163m). Arrive in Chame, administrative headquarters of Manang district.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, and Dinner Included

Accommodation: Tea House in Chame

Drive Duration: 5 hours

DAY 4 TREK TO PISANG 3,230m

First Trekking Day: Begin actual trekking with 5-hour walk to Pisang. Trail follows Marsyangdi River through pine forests. Pass villages of Bhratang and Dhukur Pokhari with increasing Tibetan Buddhist influence. Stunning views of Annapurna II and IV. Arrive in Pisang, divided into Upper and Lower sections.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, and Dinner Included

Accommodation: Tea House in Pisang

Trek Duration: 5 hours

DAY 5 TREK TO MANANG 3,450m

Heart of Manang: 5-hour trek to culturally rich Manang village. Choose between upper route via Ghyaru (challenging with better views) or lower route through Hongde (easier). Upper route offers breathtaking panoramas of Annapurna peaks. Manang features distinct Tibetan culture, ancient monasteries, and stunning mountain backdrop.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, and Dinner Included

Accommodation: Tea House in Manang

Trek Duration: 5 hours

DAY 6 ACCLIMATIZATION IN MANANG

Altitude Adjustment: Crucial rest day for body adaptation to higher altitude, reducing altitude sickness risk. Acclimatization hike to Gangapurna Lake (3,600m) or Praken Gompa viewpoint (3,900m). Both offer spectacular views and gentle exercise. Explore Manang village, visit Himalayan Rescue Association clinic, or relax. Early to bed for higher altitudes tomorrow.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, and Dinner Included

Accommodation: Tea House in Manang

Activity: Acclimatization hike (2-3 hours)

DAY 7 TREK TO YAK KHARKA 4,110m

Higher Elevations: Shorter but important ascent day climbing to Yak Kharka ("yak pasture"). 4-hour trek follows Jarsang Khola valley with landscape becoming more arid and alpine. Vegetation thins as altitude increases. Excellent views of surrounding peaks including Chulu East and West. Arrive at small Yak Kharka settlement by early afternoon for rest and acclimatization.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, and Dinner Included

Accommodation: Tea House in Yak Kharka

Trek Duration: 4 hours

DAY 8 TREK TO THORONG HIGH CAMP 4,600m

Approaching the Pass: Short but steep 4-hour climb to Thorong High Camp, base for tomorrow's pass crossing. Trail ascends steadily through rugged terrain. Pass through Thorong Phedi (4,450m). Continue to High Camp, arriving early afternoon. Use remainder of day to prepare: organize daypack, hydrate, get early night. Temperature drops significantly at this altitude.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, and Dinner Included

Accommodation: Basic Lodge at High Camp

Trek Duration: 4 hours

DAY 9 CROSS THORONG LA PASS TO MUKTINATH 5,416m (Pass) / 3,800m

The Challenge Day: Early start (4-5 AM) essential for safe pass crossing. 7-hour trek begins with steep 3-4 hour climb to Thorong La Pass (5,416m), highest point. Celebrate achievement with 360-degree Himalayan views. Begin long descent to Muktinath (3,800m) - over 1,600m down! Challenging descent rewards with changing landscapes entering Mustang region. Arrive in Muktinath exhausted but exhilarated.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, and Dinner Included

Accommodation: Lodge in Muktinath

Trek Duration: 7-8 hours

DAY 10 DRIVE TO POKHARA 810m

Return to Civilization: After breakfast, visit sacred Muktinath Temple complex important to Hindus and Buddhists. Board local deluxe bus for scenic 10-hour drive to Pokhara. Journey through dramatic Kali Gandaki Gorge (world's deepest), past Tatopani's hot springs. Notice dramatic climate and vegetation changes descending. Arrive in beautiful Pokhara by evening, check into hotel by Phewa Lake.

Meals: Breakfast and Dinner Included

Accommodation: Hotel in Pokhara

Drive Duration: 10 hours

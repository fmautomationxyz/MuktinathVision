Perfect Combination Trek

Our 10-day Annapurna Base Camp with Poon Hill trek offers the perfect balance of scenic beauty, cultural experience, and Himalayan adventure. Start with the famous Poon Hill sunrise, enjoy the rich Gurung culture in traditional villages, then venture into the spectacular Annapurna Sanctuary. This trek is ideal for those who want to experience the best of the Annapurna region without the extreme altitude of the Thorong La Pass. The well-paced itinerary includes natural hot springs, lush rhododendron forests, and close-up views of some of the world's highest peaks.

10-Day Detailed Itinerary

DAY 1 DRIVE POKHARA TO BHIRETHATI & TREK TO TIRKHEDHUNGA 1,460m

Journey Begins: Start your Annapurna Base Camp and Poon Hill 10-day trek from beautiful Pokhara, a renowned tourist destination in the Himalaya region. Drive takes you beyond Pokhara valley to rural farm villages to reach the foothills. On reaching the starting point at Bhirethati, the adventure begins with a climb of few hours to reach Tirkhedhunga, a farm village with beautiful waterfalls located on the bottom of Ulleri hill.

Meals: Lunch and Dinner Included

Accommodation: Tea House in Tirkhedhunga

Trek Duration: 5 hours

DAY 2 TREK TO GHOREPANI 2,880m

Stone Steps to Ghorepani: Morning starts with a steep long climb on stone steps to reach large Ulleri village, and then on a nice gradual winding path with dense rhododendron and pine forest to reach Ghorepani. A lovely village amid coniferous forest located on the base of Poon Hill. Enjoy the evening in this picturesque village preparing for tomorrow's sunrise hike.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, and Dinner Included

Accommodation: Tea House in Ghorepani

Trek Duration: 6 hours

DAY 3 POON HILL SUNRISE & TREK TO TADAPANI 3,210m (Poon Hill) → 2,630m

Spectacular Sunrise: Early morning before dawn hike for an hour to Poon Hill (3,210m) to catch stunning sunrise views over the Himalayan range of peaks from Dhaulagiri, Annapurna as far towards Manaslu with majestic Machhapuchare (Fish-Tail peak). Enjoying a glorious morning with fantastic views, the trek continues from Ghorepani with ups and downhill within dense rhododendron forest to reach Tadapani for overnight halt.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, and Dinner Included

Accommodation: Tea House in Tadapani

Trek Duration: 5 hours

DAY 4 TREK TO CHHORNONG VILLAGE 2,170m

Gateway to Annapurna Sanctuary: Morning easy downhill walk through forest of rhododendron, pines, and oak tree lines to reach by the river flowing from Annapurna South glacier. Cross a bridge with an hour climb above Kimrong River valley, then on gradual trail to reach Chhornong, a large Gurung village festooned with Buddhist prayer flags. Chhornong is the gateway to Annapurna Sanctuary and base camp, as well as the last permanent village on the route.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, and Dinner Included

Accommodation: Tea House in Chhornong

Trek Duration: 6 hours

DAY 5 TREK TO DOVAN VIA BAMBOO 2,630m

Entering the Sanctuary: Continue deeper into the Annapurna region, passing through bamboo forests and following the Modi River upstream. The trail becomes more rugged and remote as you approach the heart of the sanctuary. Reach Dovan for overnight stay, enjoying the increasingly dramatic mountain scenery.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, and Dinner Included

Accommodation: Tea House in Dovan

Trek Duration: 6 hours

DAY 6 TREK TO MACHHAPUCHHRE BASE CAMP 3,800m

Approaching the Fish-Tail: From Dovan, walk with short down and ups into thick tree lines to Himalaya Hotel, near Hinko Cave, then to Deurali where tree lines drop for smaller bush and shrubs. After lunch, continue with steep up to Machhapuchhre Base Camp (MBC), located beneath the towering North West Face of Machhapuchhre Himal (Fish-Tail). This lovely spot marks the start of scenic Annapurna Sanctuary with 4-5 simple lodges for overnight.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, and Dinner Included

Accommodation: Tea House at MBC

Trek Duration: 6 hours

DAY 7 TREK TO ANNAPURNA BASE CAMP 4,130m

Reaching the Sanctuary Heart: Morning short walk of few hours to reach Annapurna Base Camp, the final goal and highlight. Climb less than an hour to enter Annapurna Sanctuary proper. Located on a plateau surrounded by snow peaks on both sides. The highest point of the trek and a picturesque place within remorseless fields of ice and glaciers enclosed within towering mountains with Annapurna-I rising above. Afternoon at leisure for short hike or relaxing with breathtaking panorama.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, and Dinner Included

Accommodation: Tea House at ABC

Trek Duration: 3 hours

DAY 8 TREK TO BAMBOO 2,345m

Return Journey Begins: Enjoy breakfast with backdrop of massive Annapurna peaks, then begin return journey on same interesting route to Deurali. Leaving the sanctuary, the walk enters back into dense green vegetation and tall tree lines, following Modi River downstream to Bamboo for overnight stop.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, and Dinner Included

Accommodation: Tea House in Bamboo

Trek Duration: 6 hours

DAY 9 TREK TO JIHNU VILLAGE 1,780m

Hot Springs Relaxation: From Bamboo with short climb to Sinuwa Danda, then down to Chomrong river and up stone steps to Chomrong for lunch. Continue walking down to Jihnu village for overnight stop. Jihnu features natural hot spring pools by Modi River where you can refresh yourself after days of trekking. Perfect way to soothe tired muscles!

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, and Dinner Included

Accommodation: Tea House in Jihnu

Trek Duration: 6 hours

DAY 10 TREK TO ROAD HEAD & DRIVE TO POKHARA 860m

Return to Pokhara: Last day of adventure with short walk down to road head at Samrung Khola for exciting drive back to scenic Pokhara by Phewa Lakeside. On reaching Pokhara, check into hotel facing grand views of Annapurna and Manaslu Himalaya range with majestic Machhapuchhre. Enjoy afternoon in Pokhara after completing this incredible trekking journey.

Meals: Breakfast and Lunch Included

Accommodation: Not Included (Tour Ends in Pokhara)

Travel Duration: 3 hours trek + drive

The Ultimate Annapurna Adventure

Our 16-day Annapurna Circuit with Tilicho Lake trek is designed for serious trekkers seeking the complete Himalayan experience. This challenging but rewarding journey takes you to remote valleys, ancient villages, and some of the most spectacular mountain scenery on Earth. The addition of Tilicho Lake – a stunning turquoise glacial lake surrounded by 7,000m peaks – makes this itinerary truly special. With careful acclimatization, experienced guides, and comprehensive support, this trek offers an unforgettable adventure for those with good fitness and mountain experience.

16-Day Detailed Itinerary

DAY 1 ARRIVAL IN KATHMANDU 1,400m

Welcome to Nepal: Arrive at Tribhuvan International Airport where our representative will greet you. Transfer to your hotel in Kathmandu. After check-in, attend a comprehensive trek briefing with your guide who will review the 16-day itinerary, check your equipment, and answer questions. Evening free to explore Thamel or rest after your journey.

Meals: Welcome Dinner Included

Accommodation: Hotel in Kathmandu

DAY 2 DRIVE TO JAGAT VIA BESISAHAR 1,290m

Journey Begins: Early morning departure from Kathmandu after breakfast. Scenic 8-hour drive through rural Nepal to Besisahar (800m), then continue to Jagat (1,290m). Witness landscape transformation from urban to rural with terraced fields and traditional villages. Arrive in Jagat, check into lodge, and prepare for tomorrow's trekking adventure.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, and Dinner Included

Accommodation: Lodge in Jagat

Drive Duration: 8 hours

DAY 3 TREK TO DHARAPANI 1,920m

First Trekking Day: Begin actual trekking with 5-hour hike to Dharapani (1,920m). Trail follows Marsyangdi River through beautiful forests and traditional villages. Pass through Chamje and Tal villages, noticing gradual change in architecture and culture. Arrive in Dharapani by afternoon, a significant village at the confluence of two rivers.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, and Dinner Included

Accommodation: Tea House in Dharapani

Trek Duration: 5 hours

DAY 4 TREK TO CHAME 2,670m

District Headquarters: 5-hour trek to Chame (2,670m), administrative headquarters of Manang district. Trail continues alongside Marsyangdi River with increasingly dramatic mountain scenery. Pass through Bagarchhap, Danaque, and Timang villages. Enjoy views of Manaslu (8,163m) and Lamjung Himal. Arrive in Chame surrounded by pine forests.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, and Dinner Included

Accommodation: Tea House in Chame

Trek Duration: 5 hours

DAY 5 TREK TO PISANG 3,230m

Higher Altitudes: 5-hour trek to Pisang (3,230m) with increasing altitude. Trail offers stunning views of Annapurna II and IV. Pass through Bhratang village, famous for apple orchards, and Dhukur Pokhari. Notice distinct Tibetan Buddhist influence increasing. Arrive in Pisang, divided into Upper and Lower sections with magnificent mountain views.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, and Dinner Included

Accommodation: Tea House in Pisang

Trek Duration: 5 hours

DAY 6 TREK TO MANANG VILLAGE 3,450m

Heart of Manang: 5-hour trek to culturally rich Manang village (3,450m). Choose between upper route via Ghyaru with better views or lower route through Hongde. Upper route offers breathtaking panoramas of Annapurna peaks. Manang features distinct Tibetan culture, ancient monasteries (visit 500-year-old Barge Monastery), and stunning mountain backdrop.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, and Dinner Included

Accommodation: Tea House in Manang

Trek Duration: 5 hours

DAY 7 ACCLIMATIZATION IN MANANG

Altitude Adjustment: Crucial rest day for body adaptation. Acclimatization hike to Gangapurna Lake (3,600m) or Praken Gompa viewpoint (3,900m). Both offer spectacular views and gentle exercise. Visit Himalayan Rescue Association clinic for altitude talk. Explore Manang village, its ancient monasteries, and local culture. Early to bed preparing for higher altitudes.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, and Dinner Included

Accommodation: Tea House in Manang

Activity: Acclimatization hike (3-4 hours)

DAY 8 TREK TO KHANGSAR 3,734m

Tilicho Lake Route: 5-hour trek to Khangsar (3,734m), starting the Tilicho Lake side trip. Trail diverges from main Annapurna Circuit toward Tilicho Lake. Pass through Tengi village with views of Gangapurna and Tilicho Peak. Landscape becomes more arid and dramatic. Khangsar is the last permanent settlement before Tilicho Base Camp.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, and Dinner Included

Accommodation: Tea House in Khangsar

Trek Duration: 5 hours

DAY 9 TREK TO TILICHO BASE CAMP 3,900m

Approaching the Lake: 4-hour trek to Tilicho Base Camp (3,900m). Challenging trail through landslide-prone area with spectacular mountain views. Landscape becomes increasingly rugged and alpine. Arrive at base camp located beneath Tilicho Peak (7,134m). Rest and prepare for tomorrow's ascent to the world's highest lake.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, and Dinner Included

Accommodation: Basic Lodge at Tilicho Base Camp

Trek Duration: 4 hours

DAY 10 VISIT TILICHO LAKE & TREK TO SIRI KHARKA 4,919m (Lake) → 4,010m

Tilicho Lake Highlight: Early start for 2-3 hour steep climb to Tilicho Lake (4,919m) - the world's highest lake. Marvel at stunning turquoise waters surrounded by towering peaks including Tilicho Peak (7,134m). After photos and celebration, return to base camp and continue 6-hour trek to Siri Kharka (4,010m). Challenging but rewarding day.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, and Dinner Included

Accommodation: Tea House in Siri Kharka

Trek Duration: 6 hours total

DAY 11 TREK TO YAK KHARKA 4,110m

Rejoining Main Circuit: 5-hour trek to Yak Kharka (4,110m), rejoining main Annapurna Circuit. Trail descends then climbs through increasingly arid alpine terrain. Notice vegetation thinning as altitude increases. Excellent views of surrounding peaks including Chulu East and West. Arrive at Yak Kharka ("yak pasture") by afternoon.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, and Dinner Included

Accommodation: Tea House in Yak Kharka

Trek Duration: 5 hours

DAY 12 TREK TO THORONG HIGH CAMP 4,600m

Approaching the Pass: 4-hour climb to Thorong High Camp (4,600m), base for tomorrow's pass crossing. Trail ascends steadily through rugged terrain. Pass through Thorong Phedi (4,450m). Continue to High Camp, arriving early afternoon. Use remaining time to prepare for tomorrow: organize daypack, hydrate, get early night. Temperature drops significantly.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, and Dinner Included

Accommodation: Basic Lodge at High Camp

Trek Duration: 4 hours

DAY 13 CROSS THORONG LA PASS TO MUKTINATH 5,416m (Pass) / 3,800m

The Challenge Day: Early start (4-5 AM) for safe pass crossing. 7-hour trek begins with steep 3-4 hour climb to Thorong La Pass (5,416m), highest point. Celebrate achievement with prayer flags and 360-degree Himalayan views. Begin long descent to Muktinath (3,800m) - over 1,600m down! Arrive exhausted but exhilarated at this sacred site.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, and Dinner Included

Accommodation: Lodge in Muktinath

Trek Duration: 7 hours

DAY 14 FLY OR DRIVE TO POKHARA 800m

Options Available: After breakfast, visit sacred Muktinath Temple. Choose between scenic 10-hour drive or 25-minute flight to Pokhara (flight cost additional). Drive through Kali Gandaki Gorge (world's deepest), past Tatopani hot springs. Flight offers spectacular aerial views. Arrive in Pokhara, check into hotel by Phewa Lake, enjoy hot shower and celebration.

Meals: Breakfast and Dinner Included

Accommodation: Hotel in Pokhara

Travel Duration: 10 hours drive or 25-minute flight

DAY 15 RETURN TO KATHMANDU 1,400m

Options Available: Choose between scenic 6-7 hour drive or 25-minute flight to Kathmandu (flight cost additional). Drive offers beautiful rural views; flight provides spectacular Himalayan aerial views. Arrive in Kathmandu, transfer to hotel. Evening free for relaxation, shopping, or exploring. Farewell dinner with trekking team to celebrate your incredible achievement.

Meals: Breakfast and Farewell Dinner Included

Accommodation: Hotel in Kathmandu

Travel Duration: 6-7 hours drive or 25-minute flight

DAY 16 FINAL DEPARTURE

Journey Completion: After breakfast, check out from hotel (standard check-out 12 PM). Our representative will assist with airport transfers according to flight schedule. Depart with incredible memories of Himalayan vistas, Tilicho Lake's turquoise waters, Thorong La Pass achievement, cultural encounters, and personal triumph. Carry hope to return to Nepal's majestic mountains.

Meals: Breakfast Included

Until we meet again in the Himalayas! Safe travels!

Tour Concludes.

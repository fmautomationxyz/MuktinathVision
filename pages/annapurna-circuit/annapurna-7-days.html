Perfect for Experienced Trekkers

Our 7-day Annapurna Circuit Express is ideal for experienced trekkers who are short on time but high on adventure. This challenging itinerary requires good physical fitness and some high-altitude experience. 7-Day Detailed Itinerary

DAY 1 ARRIVAL IN KATHMANDU & DRIVE TO CHAME 2,670m

Arrival & Direct Transfer: Arrive at Tribhuvan International Airport. Our representative will greet you and provide immediate transfer to local bus for journey to Besisahar (approx 7-8 hours). At Besisahar, change to sharing jeep for drive to Chame (approx 5 hours). Arrive in Chame, administrative headquarters of Manang district (2,670m). Brief trek orientation and overnight in tea house.

Meals: Dinner Included

Accommodation: Tea House in Chame

Travel Duration: 12-13 hours total

DAY 2 TREK TO UPPER PISANG 3,300m / 10,827ft

First Trekking Day: Begin trekking with challenging 7-8 hour hike to Upper Pisang. Trail follows Marsyangdi River through beautiful pine forests. Pass traditional villages with increasing Tibetan Buddhist influence. Upper Pisang offers magnificent views of the Annapurna range and Pisang Peak. Notice dramatic change in architecture and culture. Arrive at Upper Pisang (3,300m) by evening.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, and Dinner Included

Accommodation: Tea House in Upper Pisang

Trek Duration: 7-8 hours

DAY 3 TREK TO MANANG 3,540m / 11,614ft

To Manang Village: 6-7 hour trek to culturally rich Manang village (3,540m). Continue through increasingly dramatic mountain scenery. The trail offers breathtaking panoramas of Annapurna II, III, IV, and Gangapurna. Manang is a major settlement with distinct Tibetan culture, ancient monasteries, and stunning mountain backdrop. Explore the village and acclimatize to the increasing altitude upon arrival.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, and Dinner Included

Accommodation: Tea House in Manang

Trek Duration: 6-7 hours

DAY 4 TREK TO LEDAR 4,200m / 13,123ft

Higher Elevations: 4-5 hour trek to Ledar (4,200m). The landscape becomes more arid and alpine as you gain altitude. Trail follows the Jarsang Khola valley with excellent views of surrounding peaks including Chulu East and West. Notice vegetation thinning significantly. Arrive at Ledar by early afternoon, allowing time for acclimatization. This gradual ascent is crucial for preparing for higher altitudes.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, and Dinner Included

Accommodation: Tea House in Ledar

Trek Duration: 4-5 hours

DAY 5 TREK TO HIGH CAMP 4,870m / 15,748ft

Approaching the Pass: Challenging 5-6 hour climb to High Camp (4,870m), your base for tomorrow's pass crossing. Trail ascends steadily through rugged, high-altitude terrain. Pass through Thorong Phedi (4,450m). Continue steep ascent to High Camp, arriving by afternoon. Use remaining time to prepare: organize daypack, hydrate well, and get early night. Temperature drops significantly at this altitude. Critical day for acclimatization.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, and Dinner Included

Accommodation: Basic Lodge at High Camp

Trek Duration: 5-6 hours

DAY 6 CROSS THORONG LA PASS TO MUKTINATH 5,416m / 17,769ft (Pass) → 3,800m

The Challenge Day: Very early start (around 3-4 AM) essential for safe pass crossing. 7-8 hour trek begins with steep 4-5 hour climb to Thorong La Pass (5,416m), highest point of trek. Celebrate achievement at pass with prayer flags and 360-degree Himalayan views. Begin long descent to Muktinath (3,800m) - over 1,600m down! Challenging descent rewards with changing landscapes entering Mustang region. Arrive in Muktinath exhausted but exhilarated.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, and Dinner Included

Accommodation: Lodge in Muktinath

Trek Duration: 7-8 hours

DAY 7 DRIVE TO POKHARA 800m

Return to Civilization: After breakfast, visit sacred Muktinath Temple complex important to Hindus and Buddhists. Board local deluxe bus for scenic 8-9 hour drive (200km) to Pokhara. Journey through dramatic Kali Gandaki Gorge (world's deepest), past Tatopani's hot springs. Notice dramatic climate and vegetation changes descending. Arrive in beautiful Pokhara by evening. Tour concludes in Pokhara. Optional: Additional night in Pokhara or return to Kathmandu (not included).

Meals: Breakfast Included

Accommodation: Not Included (Tour Ends)

Drive Duration: 8-9 hours

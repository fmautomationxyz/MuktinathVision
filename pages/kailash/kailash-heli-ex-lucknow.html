Detailed Itinerary

DAY 01 PICKUP FROM LUCKNOW. NEPALGUNJ ARRIVAL

Pick from Lucknow. Arrival at Nepalgunj. Transfer to a Hotel in Nepalgunj or Drive to Surkhet and stay at Surkhet based on the situation.

Meal: Dinner at Nepalgunj Hotel

Hotel: Hotel Sneha or similar

DAY 02 FLY NEPALGUNJ - SIMIKOT (2900m) - OVERNIGHT IN SIMIKOT 2,900m

Early Morning – Flight Nepalgunj to Simikot (1hr). Rest day at Simikot.

Meal: Breakfast, Lunch & Dinner

Hotel: New Potala Palace or Similar on sharing basis

DAY 03 HELI TO HILSA (3700m) & DRIVE TO TAKLAKOT (3900m) 3,900m

Morning – Take Helicopter to Hilsa (20 min – 12kg weight limit) & wait for other members to arrive at Hilsa (since it is a group visa and everyone have to enter Tibet side in one group).

Afternoon – From Hilsa drive to Purang border to Taklakot (27 km) and stay at a Hotel. We will stay overnight at Taklakot (on the way we have to pass up to 3 Checkpoints where checking might take up to 1 hr in each post).

Meal: Breakfast, Lunch & Dinner

Hotel: Hotel Himalaya, New Purang, Riverside or Similar

DAY 04 AT TAKLAKOT (3900m) - REST DAY 3,900m

All Day – Entire day free for Acclimatization.

Meal: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Hotel: Hotel Himalaya, New Purang, Riverside or Similar

DAY 05 DRIVE TO MANASAROVAR LAKE (4550m) 4,550m

After Breakfast – Drive towards Manasarovar Lake (95 km). On the way you will have to pass the first checkpoint. Then after driving for 70 km from Taklakot we will reach Rakshas Tal. On the right of Rakshas Tal you will see Gurla Mandhata Parvat & first view of Mount Kailash and Manasarovar lake. Drive from Rakshas Tal to Manasarovar Lake (25 km).

Start Parikrama of Manasarovar Lake (90 km – 3hr by Private A/C Coach). Perform Puja.

Meal: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Hotel: Lake Guesthouse

DAY 06 1ST DAY OF PARIKRAMA - TREK TO DIRAPUK (4880m) 4,880m

After Breakfast – Drive to Darchen (35 km – 1hr) and Yama Dwar (8 km) – the starting point for Kora. We will trek to Dirapuk for 12 km (5 to 7hrs).

Travelers who do not want to trek around (Parikrama) Mt. Kailash can go back to Darchen and wait for the rest of the group to arrive from Parikrama.

Meal: Breakfast at Manasarovar, Packed Lunch & Dinner

Hotel: Guesthouse on the way during Parikrama or Stay in Dormitory Guest House at Darchen

NOTE: If you are not doing Parikrama, then you will still be waiting at Darchen (4650m)
DAY 07 2ND DAY OF PARIKRAMA - VIA DOLMA LA PASS (5700m) TO ZUTHULPHUK (4670m) 5,700m (Dolma La)

07:00AM – Trek from Dirapuk to Dolma La Pass and proceed to Zuthulphuk (22 km trek for 8-9 hours trek). The first stage of the trip is a two-hour ascent to Dolma La Pass (5700 m | 18700 ft). The trail brings us past Yamasthal on our way to Shiva-sthal, with each step bringing us closer to the Dolma-La Pass.

There are some tea shops before you reach Dolma La Pass where you can have tea and hot refreshments. After Dolma La Pass, the route descends for an hour before straightening out for 2-3 hours till we reach Zuthulphuk. You'll be able to see Gauri Kunda (The Pond of Goddess Parvati) as well while descending from Dolma la pass.

Meal: Breakfast, Packed Lunch, Dinner

Hotel: Guesthouse

NOTE: If you are not doing Parikrama, then you will still be waiting at Darchen
DAY 08 3RD DAY OF PARIKRAMA - RETURN TO HILSA / SIMIKOT 3,700m

After Breakfast – After a short easy Trek (8 km – 3hrs), we will reach our Private A/C Coach pick up point. Then drive to Darchen (3 km) and merge with other members who were waiting. From there drive to Hilsa (122 kms – 4 to 5hrs including immigration and customs). Also we might take a helicopter to Simikot based on the situation.

Meal: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Hotel: Mansarovar Guest House or Similar

DAY 09 FLY TO NEPALGUNJ - TRANSFER TO LUCKNOW

Fly to Simikot. Fly from Simikot to Nepalgunj / Surkhet and proceed to Lucknow.

Meal: Breakfast at Simikot

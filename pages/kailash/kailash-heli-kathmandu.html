Detailed Itinerary

DAY 1 ARRIVAL IN KATHMANDU 1,400m

At Arrival Time: Upon arrival at Tribhuvan International Airport in Kathmandu, you will be warmly welcomed by our representative holding a placard with your name. After completing immigration and customs formalities, transfer to your luxurious 5 Star hotel in the heart of Kathmandu. Check-in and freshen up after your journey. In the evening, gather for a welcome dinner where you'll meet your fellow pilgrims and receive a comprehensive briefing about the sacred journey ahead. This is an opportunity to ask questions and understand what to expect in the coming days.

Meals: Dinner Included

Accommodation: 5 Star Hotel in Kathmandu

DAY 2 KATHMANDU SIGHTSEEING & BRIEFING 1,400m

After breakfast, embark on a guided spiritual tour of Kathmandu's most sacred UNESCO World Heritage Sites. Visit the revered Pashupatinath Temple, one of the most important Hindu temples dedicated to Lord Shiva, where you can witness traditional Hindu cremation ceremonies on the banks of the holy Bagmati River. Continue to Boudhanath Stupa, one of the largest Buddhist stupas in the world, and experience the peaceful atmosphere as you walk clockwise around it with pilgrims from around the world. Explore the ancient Swayambhunath (Monkey Temple) perched atop a hill with panoramic views of the Kathmandu Valley. Afternoon session for final preparations, permit verification, and detailed briefing about altitude sickness prevention and trek preparations.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, and Dinner Included

Accommodation: 5 Star Hotel in Kathmandu

DAY 3 HELICOPTER TO NEPALGUNJ 150m

Early morning transfer to the domestic terminal for your helicopter flight to Nepalgunj. The helicopter journey offers breathtaking aerial views of the Kathmandu Valley, terraced hillsides, rivers, and the gradually changing landscape as you approach the Terai plains. Upon arrival in Nepalgunj, transfer to your comfortable 3 Star hotel. The afternoon is dedicated to rest and acclimatization. Our team will provide comprehensive information about the upcoming journey, conduct a final gear check, and ensure all permits and documents are in order.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, and Dinner Included

Accommodation: 3 Star Hotel in Nepalgunj

Flight Duration: 1 hour

DAY 4 FLY TO HILSA & DRIVE TO LAKE MANSAROVAR 4,590m

This is one of the most significant and transformative days of your journey. Take an early morning helicopter flight from Nepalgunj to Simikot, flying over the stunning Himalayan foothills and remote villages. From Simikot, continue by helicopter to Hilsa at the Nepal-Tibet border, experiencing dramatic changes in landscape as you enter the Tibetan plateau. After completing immigration and customs formalities at the border, embark on a scenic drive through the vast, barren beauty of the Tibetan plateau to reach the sacred Lake Mansarovar. Upon arrival at this holiest of lakes, perform the holy dip in the crystal-clear waters (weather and health permitting) and participate in Puja ceremonies on the banks.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, and Dinner Included

Accommodation: Guest House near Mansarovar

Flight Duration: 2 hours (via Simikot)

Drive Duration: 3-4 hours

DAY 5 MANSAROVAR PARIKRAMA & DRIVE TO DARCHEN 4,600m

After morning prayers and rituals at Lake Mansarovar, you have the option to perform a partial or full Parikrama (circumambulation) of the sacred lake by vehicle. The lake circuit of approximately 90 km offers stunning views from different angles and vantage points, each one more breathtaking than the last. Later, drive to Darchen, the base camp for Mount Kailash Parikrama. The drive offers spectacular first views of the majestic Mount Kailash from various angles. Upon arrival in Darchen, rest and prepare both physically and mentally for the challenging three-day Parikrama ahead.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, and Dinner Included

Accommodation: Guest House in Darchen

Drive Duration: 2 hours

DAY 6 BEGIN KAILASH PARIKRAMA - TREK TO DERAPHUK 5,000m

Begin your sacred Kailash Parikrama early morning from Darchen after prayers and blessings. The trail follows the Lha-chu valley with Mount Kailash's majestic western face visible throughout the day, a constant source of inspiration and spiritual energy. Trek through dramatic landscapes with colorful prayer flags fluttering in the mountain breeze. Pass by significant religious sites including ancient monasteries, sacred stones, and traditional pilgrimage markers. Reach Deraphuk monastery by late afternoon where you'll have stunning close-up views of Mount Kailash's magnificent North Face.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, and Dinner Included

Accommodation: Guest House at Deraphuk

Trek Duration: 5-6 hours

Trek Distance: 12 km

DAY 7 TREK VIA DOLMA LA PASS TO ZHUTHULPHUK 5,630m

This is the most challenging and spiritually significant day of the Parikrama. Start very early (around 3-4 AM) to reach Dolma La Pass at 5,630m, the highest point of the entire trek. The ascent is steep and physically demanding, but immensely rewarding spiritually. According to tradition, crossing this pass symbolizes death and rebirth, a spiritual transformation. At the pass, offer prayers at the sacred Dolma La stone covered with prayer flags. The views from the top are absolutely breathtaking. The descent is equally challenging, requiring careful footing on rocky terrain. Pass by the sacred emerald-green Gauri Kund lake, believed to have been created by Goddess Parvati. Continue descending through the valley to reach Zhuthulphuk by evening.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, and Dinner Included

Accommodation: Guest House at Zhuthulphuk

Trek Duration: 8-10 hours

Trek Distance: 18 km

Note: Personal riding horses available for rent (not included in package)

DAY 8 COMPLETE PARIKRAMA & RETURN TO HILSA 3,700m

Complete the final leg of your sacred Kailash Parikrama. The trail descends gradually through beautiful valleys with magnificent views of Mount Kailash from the eastern side. After completing the full 52km circuit around Mount Kailash, feel a profound sense of spiritual accomplishment. According to belief, completing this Parikrama once washes away the sins of one lifetime. Drive back to Hilsa at the Nepal-Tibet border, retracing your route across the Tibetan plateau. Complete exit formalities from Tibet. Rest and celebrate with your group the successful completion of this sacred pilgrimage.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, and Dinner Included

Accommodation: Guest House at Hilsa

Trek Duration: 3-4 hours

Drive Duration: 4-5 hours

DAY 9 FLY TO KATHMANDU VIA NEPALGUNJ 1,400m

Take helicopter from Hilsa to Simikot, enjoying your last aerial views of the Himalayan landscape. From Simikot, continue your helicopter journey to Nepalgunj. Upon arrival in Nepalgunj, you have the option of either taking a connecting flight or driving to Kathmandu (depending on package arrangements and weather conditions). Upon arrival in Kathmandu, transfer to your luxury 5 Star hotel. Enjoy the comforts of modern amenities after days of basic accommodation in the mountains. The evening is free for rest, shopping, or exploring Kathmandu's streets and restaurants.

Meals: Breakfast and Dinner Included

Accommodation: 5 Star Hotel in Kathmandu

Flight Duration: 3-4 hours total

DAY 10 DEPARTURE FROM KATHMANDU

After breakfast, depending on your flight schedule, you may have some free time for last-minute shopping or sightseeing in Kathmandu. Our representative will assist you with check-out procedures and transfer you to Tribhuvan International Airport for your onward journey home. We'll help with check-in formalities and bid you farewell. Depart with blessed memories, spiritual fulfillment, and divine grace from your sacred Kailash Mansarovar Yatra.

Meals: Breakfast Included

May Lord Shiva's blessings be with you always! Om Namah Shivaya! 🙏

Detailed Itinerary - 17 Days

DAY 1 ARRIVAL IN KATHMANDU 1,400m

Welcome to Himalayan kingdom of Nepal. You will be met on airport and transferred to your hotel. After the arrival, we will be briefing about your trip. If you have any questions or doubts about your trip should be asked and clarified at this time. Then evening you will be visit to Pashupati Nath Temple for Aarati Darshan, than back to Hotel. Overnight.

Meals: Welcome Dinner Included

Accommodation: 3 Star Hotel in Kathmandu

DAY 2 DRIVE FROM KATHMANDU TO POKHARA 827m

Journey to Pokhara: You have breakfast being of the early morning. This morning you will be drive to Pokhara (200km), departure at 7:00 AM, approximately six hours away by tourist coach. En route, you could find Trisuli River, it is very famous for Himalayan wild river rafting and also you can find Manakamana temple, can be visit by cable car it is a 1,302-m hill. The deity is one of the manifestations of the Hindu Goddess Bhagwati who is believed to have the power to fulfill wishes. It is one of the most popular pilgrimage sites in Nepal. You will be arriving late afternoon at Pokhara. Pokhara is tourist's paradise with full of natural as well as cultural heritage sites such as lakes, caves, temples of Buddhist and Hindus along with mountains. You can observe views to the north across the hills and Phewa Tal (lake) to the white peaks of the Annapurna and Dhaulagiri ranges it is most beautiful Himalayan panoramas in the world.

Option: You can also fly from Kathmandu to Pokhara which takes about 25 minutes (extra cost)

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, and Dinner Included

Accommodation: 3 Star Hotel in Pokhara

Drive Duration: 6-7 hours

DAY 3 DRIVE TO MUKTINATH, STAY IN JOMSOM 3,710m

Muktinath Pilgrimage: Early morning, commence our today's adventurous journey. From Pokhara, Muktinath is 195 km and takes 6/7 hours drive. Arrival at Muktinath and hike ascending for 30/40 mins upto the temple. Reach at Muktinath Kshetra and visit Samba Gompa, Muktinath Yagyashala, Vishnu Paduka, Mukteshwor Mahadev Temple, and Muktinath Temple. After reaching Muktinath Temple, commence our ritual activities. Take holy bath under 108 Mukti Dhara and 2 Mukti Kundas. After Muktinath Mandir Darshan, visit Jwalamai Temple. After Darshan and Pooja, drive back to Jomsom for a night's stay.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, and Dinner Included

Accommodation: Tourist standard accommodation in Jomsom

Drive Duration: 6-7 hours

Trek Duration: 30-40 minutes uphill

DAY 4 DRIVE TO POKHARA, POKHARA SIGHTSEEING 827m

Return to Pokhara: At. 07:00 AM: After your breakfast, you will depart for Pokhara, stopping at some of the key attractions on the route back. First, you'll visit Kali Gandaki River, Rupse Waterfall and Galeshwor Mahadev Temple. At. 02:30 PM: Today's half day-sightseeing visit to Bindabasini Temple, Devi's Fall, Gupteshwor Mahadev Cave and Tal Barahi Temple at Fewa Lake Back to the hotel and free time.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, and Dinner Included

Accommodation: 3 Star Hotel in Pokhara

Drive Duration: 6-7 hours

Sightseeing: Half day

DAY 5 DRIVE FROM POKHARA TO KATHMANDU 1,400m

Return to Kathmandu: You have breakfast being of the early morning. Then drive from Pokhara to Kathmandu by tourist coach, which takes about six hours. While driving from Pokhara to Kathmandu, En route, you could enjoy the mountain views, green sceneries, rice terrace fields, vegetable fields and people being engaged in their daily life activities. From Naubishe you climb up to Thankot, the gateway to capital city.

Option: You can also fly from Pokhara to Kathmandu which takes about 25 minutes (extra cost)

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, and Dinner Included

Accommodation: 3 Star Hotel in Kathmandu

Drive Duration: 6 hours

DAY 6 KATHMANDU SIGHTSEEING 1,400m

Heritage Tour: After breakfast, we start an interesting tour around Kathmandu city. Escorting by an English speaking guide (we can provide any language speaking guide on your request), try to give them a full taste of our culture, heritage and local people. In our sightseeing tour we go to Pashupatinath Temple, Jaal Narayan (Budhanilkantha), Swayambhunath (Monkey temple) and Kathmandu Durbar square.

Meals: Breakfast and Lunch Included

Accommodation: 3 Star Hotel in Kathmandu

Sightseeing: Full day

DAY 7 DRIVE FROM KATHMANDU TO NYALAM 3,700m

Entering Tibet: After breakfast depart from Kathmandu to China border (Kodari) by Private coach. It takes about 05 hours. Our staff will complete custom formalities and check into Tibet - an autonomous part of China. After lunch drive to a small Tibetan town called Nyalam by Land Cruiser. Dinner & Overnight stay at Nyalam Guest House.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, and Dinner Included

Accommodation: Guest House in Nyalam

Drive Duration: 5 hours to border + transfer to Nyalam

DAY 8 DRIVE FROM NYALAM TO DONGBA/PARYANG 4,500m

Tibetan Plateau Journey: After breakfast drive from Nyalam to Dongba/Paryang for about 08:00 hours. Overnight stay at guesthouse.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, and Dinner Included

Accommodation: Guest House at Dongba/Paryang

Drive Duration: 8 hours

DAY 9 DRIVE FROM DONGBA/PARYANG TO MANSAROVAR 4,590m

Sacred Lake Arrival: After breakfast drive from Dongba/paryang to Mansarovar 07:00 hours. Pooja and hawan, stay at the bank of the Manasarovar Lake, Overnight stay in tent or guesthouse.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, and Dinner Included

Accommodation: Tent or Guest House near Mansarovar

Drive Duration: 7 hours

DAY 10 DRIVE FROM MANSAROVAR TO DARCHEN 4,600m

Mansarovar Parikrama: After breakfast, Mansarovar Parikrama by Land Cruiser. We will choose the special point to take dip in Manasarovar Lake and do Pooja of Lord Shiva. Full day free for spiritual activities. Drive around 2.30 hrs to Darchen and overnight stay at guesthouse.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, and Dinner Included

Accommodation: Guest House in Darchen

Activities: Holy dip, Puja, Mansarovar Parikrama

DAY 11 KAILASH PARIKRAMA DAY 1: DARCHEN TO DIRAPHUK 4,890m

Begin Kailash Parikrama: This is the first day of Kailash Parikrama. We should start our journey after breakfast as early as possible. This day we have to walk continuously for about 6 to 7 hours. Yak will carry all our goods and equipment. Overnight stay at Diraphuk in tent or guesthouse.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, and Dinner Included

Accommodation: Tent or Guest House at Diraphuk

Trek Duration: 6-7 hours

Distance: 12km

DAY 12 KAILASH PARIKRAMA DAY 2: DIRAPHUK TO ZHULTHULPHUK VIA DOLMA LA 5,630m

Most Challenging Day: This day is very sensitive and we have to pass Dolma-La which is 5400m, The weather in unpredictable in Doma-la Pass and at any time snowstorm can fall. So we try to start our journey as early as possible. In route we can have Darshan of Gaurikund but it is very difficult to go and touch. Overnight stay in Zhulthulphuk in tent or Guest House.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, and Dinner Included

Accommodation: Tent or Guest House at Zhulthulphuk

Trek Duration: 8-10 hours

Distance: 22km

Note: Riding Yak/Horse available for rent (not included)

DAY 13 PARIKRAMA COMPLETION & RETURN TO MANSAROVAR 4,590m

Complete Sacred Circle: Trek down to Darchen, Drive to Mansarovar fill up spiritual jaal, then back to Kathmandu following the same route and same system applies for all subjects of our accommodation and meal.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, and Dinner Included

Accommodation: Tent or Guest House near Mansarovar

DAY 14 DRIVE BACK FROM MANSAROVAR TO DONGBA/SAGA 4,500m

Return Journey Begins: Drive back from Mansarovar to Dongba/Saga.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, and Dinner Included

Accommodation: Guest House at Dongba/Saga

DAY 15 DRIVE BACK FROM DONGBA/SAGA TO NYALAM 3,700m

Final Night in Tibet: Drive back to Nyalam, which is the last stay in Tibet part.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, and Dinner Included

Accommodation: Guest House in Nyalam

DAY 16 DRIVE BACK FROM NYALAM TO KATHMANDU 1,400m

Return to Nepal: After custom formalities you will be picked up by Nepal side and continue drive to Kathmandu for about 05.00 hours and check into same hotel.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, and Dinner Included

Accommodation: 3 Star Hotel in Kathmandu

Drive Duration: 5 hours

DAY 17 FINAL DEPARTURE

Journey Ends: After breakfast you will have some free time until the time to leave the international airport for your final departure.

Meals: Breakfast Included

Om Namah Shivaya! May Lord Shiva's and Lord Vishnu's blessings be with you always!

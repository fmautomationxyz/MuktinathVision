Kailash Mansarovar Yatra 2026 Overland

13 Days - Traditional Overland Route - For Indian Citizens

Journey Overview

Embark on a spiritually transformative 13-day overland pilgrimage to Mount Kailash and Lake Mansarovar, specially designed for Indian citizens. This traditional route offers an authentic pilgrimage experience through breathtaking landscapes of Nepal and Tibet.

Key Highlights

  • Traditional Overland Route: Authentic pilgrimage experience via Nepalgunj-Simikot-Hilsa
  • Pure Vegetarian Meals: All meals prepared with devotion and care throughout the journey
  • Extended Lake Stay: Overnight stay at sacred Lake Mansarovar for deep spiritual immersion
  • Complete Parikrama: Full 52km circumambulation of Mount Kailash with proper support
  • Cultural Immersion: Visit UNESCO World Heritage sites in Kathmandu before the journey
  • Hindi/English Guides: Experienced bilingual guides for seamless communication
  • Gradual Acclimatization: Carefully planned itinerary for altitude adjustment

Trip Facts

  • Duration: 12 Nights 13 Days
  • Route: Kathmandu → Nepalgunj → Simikot → Hilsa → Tibet → Mansarovar → Kailash → Return
  • Maximum Altitude: 5,630m (Dolma La Pass)
  • Difficulty Level: Moderate to Strenuous
  • Trek Distance: 52km Kailash Parikrama over 3 days
  • Best Season: May to September 2026
  • Group Size: 15-25 pilgrims per group
  • Special Feature: Designed specifically for Indian pilgrims

Detailed Itinerary

DAY 1 ARRIVAL IN KATHMANDU 1,400m

Welcome to Nepal: Arrive at Tribhuvan International Airport. Our representative will greet you with traditional welcome. Transfer to hotel for check-in. Evening orientation session about the journey, altitude sickness prevention, and Q&A. Welcome dinner featuring authentic Nepali cuisine.

Meals: Welcome Dinner Included

Accommodation: 5 Star Hotel in Kathmandu

DAY 2 KATHMANDU SIGHTSEEING & PREPARATION

Spiritual Preparation: Morning visit to Pashupatinath Temple for blessings. Continue to Boudhanath Stupa (UNESCO site). After lunch, visit Swayambhunath (Monkey Temple). Afternoon dedicated to finalizing permits, medical check-ups, and shopping. Evening free for personal preparations.

Meals: Breakfast and Lunch Included

Accommodation: 5 Star Hotel in Kathmandu

Sightseeing: 5-6 hours

DAY 3 FLY TO NEPALGUNJ 150m

Gateway to West Nepal: Early morning flight to Nepalgunj (1 hour). Check into hotel. Afternoon briefing about border crossing. Evening free to explore local markets or rest.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, and Dinner Included

Accommodation: 3 Star Hotel in Nepalgunj

Flight Duration: 1 hour

DAY 4 FLY TO SIMIKOT & TREK TO HILSA 3,700m

Entering the Himalayas: Helicopter flight to Simikot (45 minutes). Scenic 4-5 hour trek to Hilsa at Nepal-Tibet border. Complete immigration formalities. Overnight at border guest house.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, and Dinner Included

Accommodation: Basic Guest House at Hilsa

Flight Duration: 45 minutes

Trek Duration: 4-5 hours

DAY 5 DRIVE TO LAKE MANSAROVAR 4,590m

Sacred Arrival: Cross into Tibet and meet Tibetan guide. Scenic drive to Lake Mansarovar. Perform holy dip (weather permitting) and Puja ceremonies. Witness sunset over the lake with Kailash backdrop.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, and Dinner Included

Accommodation: Guest House near Mansarovar

Drive Duration: 4-5 hours

DAY 6 MANSAROVAR TO DARCHEN 4,600m

Base Camp Preparation: Sunrise meditation at Mansarovar. Drive to Darchen with spectacular Kailash views. Check into guest house. Detailed briefing about 3-day Parikrama trek. Prepare backpack for trek.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, and Dinner Included

Accommodation: Guest House in Darchen

Drive Duration: 2 hours

DAY 7 KAILASH PARIKRAMA DAY 1: DARCHEN TO DERAPHUK 5,000m

Begin Sacred Walk: Trek follows Lha-chu valley with Kailash's western face visible. Visit Chuku Monastery. Moderate 5-6 hour trek to Deraphuk. Spectacular North Face view of Kailash. Evening prayers and meditation.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, and Dinner Included

Accommodation: Guest House at Deraphuk

Trek Duration: 5-6 hours

Trek Distance: 12 km

DAY 8 KAILASH PARIKRAMA DAY 2: VIA DOLMA LA PASS 5,630m (Dolma La)

Most Challenging Day: Start early (3-4 AM) to reach Dolma La Pass (5,630m). Steep ascent tests endurance. At pass, offer prayers and tie prayer flags. Descent to Zhuthulphuk ("miracle cave"). Tests physical and spiritual determination.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, and Dinner Included

Accommodation: Guest House at Zhuthulphuk

Trek Duration: 8-10 hours

Trek Distance: 18 km

Note: Personal riding horses available for rent (not included)

DAY 9 KAILASH PARIKRAMA DAY 3: COMPLETE CIRCLE & RETURN 4,600m

Complete Sacred Circle: Final leg of Parikrama with gentle descent. Complete 52km sacred circle at Darchen. Drive back to Lake Mansarovar for overnight. Evening prayer and reflection by sacred lake.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, and Dinner Included

Accommodation: Guest House near Mansarovar

Trek Duration: 3-4 hours

Drive Duration: 4-5 hours

DAY 10 DRIVE TO HILSA & TREK TO SIMIKOT 3,700m

Return Journey Begins: Drive from Mansarovar to Hilsa border. Cross back into Nepal. Trek from Hilsa to Simikot (mostly downhill). Overnight in Simikot, celebrating successful pilgrimage.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, and Dinner Included

Accommodation: Guest House in Simikot

Drive Duration: 4-5 hours

Trek Duration: 4-5 hours

DAY 11 FLY TO NEPALGUNJ & DRIVE TO KATHMANDU 1,400m

Return to Civilization: Helicopter flight from Simikot to Nepalgunj. Flight or drive to Kathmandu. Check into 5-star hotel. Evening free for rest, shopping, or exploration. Celebration dinner.

Meals: Breakfast and Dinner Included

Accommodation: 5 Star Hotel in Kathmandu

Flight Duration: 45 minutes

DAY 12 KATHMANDU - FREE DAY

Rest & Reflection: Complete free day in Kathmandu. Optional activities include visiting Patan Durbar Square, Bhaktapur, or shopping. Evening farewell dinner to share experiences with fellow pilgrims.

Meals: Breakfast and Farewell Dinner Included

Accommodation: 5 Star Hotel in Kathmandu

DAY 13 DEPARTURE FROM KATHMANDU

Journey Ends: After breakfast, transfer to airport for onward journey. Our representative assists with check-in. Depart with blessed memories and spiritual fulfillment from this sacred pilgrimage.

Meals: Breakfast Included

May Lord Shiva's blessings be with you always! Om Namah Shivaya!

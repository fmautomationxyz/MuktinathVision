Important Altitude Information

The journey reaches a maximum altitude of 5,600m at Dolma La Pass. Proper acclimatization is built into the itinerary. Previous experience with high altitude is recommended. Our Nepalese guides are trained to handle altitude-related issues and provide appropriate support throughout the pilgrimage.

Why Nepalese Choose This Journey

As fellow Himalayan people, Nepalese pilgrims have a special connection with this sacred journey. The route follows ancient trade and pilgrimage paths that our ancestors have traveled for centuries. This package recognizes our unique position and offers arrangements that respect our cultural practices while ensuring a comfortable spiritual journey.

Detailed Itinerary

DAY 1 KATHMANDU TO SYABRUBESI/TIMURE 1,850m

Journey Commences: Assemble at the designated meeting point in Kathmandu early morning. After group introductions and final briefing, board our comfortable vehicle for the scenic drive northward. The route follows the Trisuli River through beautiful landscapes of terraced fields and traditional villages. Stop at Trishuli Bazar for lunch at a local restaurant. Continue driving through increasingly mountainous terrain, arriving at Syabrubesi (119km/5 hours) or Timure (124km/6 hours) depending on road conditions. Check into your hotel, enjoy dinner, and rest for the adventure ahead.

Meals: Lunch and Dinner Included

Accommodation: Local Hotel in Syabrubesi/Timure

Drive Duration: 5-6 hours

DAY 2 CROSS BORDER TO KYIRONG 2,850m

Entering Tibet: After breakfast, complete Nepal immigration formalities at Timure border post. Walk approximately 1.7km to Rasuwagadhi, the Nepal-China border crossing point. Complete customs formalities on the Nepal side before crossing the friendship bridge into Tibet. Meet your Tibetan guide who will assist with China immigration procedures. Once formalities are complete, board Tibetan vehicles for the 40km drive to Kyirong (approximately 1.5 hours). The landscape transforms as you enter the Tibetan plateau. Check into your hotel in Kyirong, rest, and acclimatize to the new environment.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, and Dinner Included

Accommodation: Hotel in Kyirong

Drive Duration: 1.5 hours

DAY 3 ACCLIMATIZATION DAY IN KYIRONG

Altitude Adjustment: Today is dedicated to acclimatization, crucial for preventing altitude sickness. Enjoy breakfast at your hotel prepared by our kitchen crew. Explore Kyirong town, visit local markets, and observe daily Tibetan life. Optional walk to nearby viewpoints or monasteries to help your body adjust to the altitude. Our guides will conduct a health check and provide advice on altitude adjustment. This rest day significantly improves comfort and safety for the higher altitudes ahead. Return to hotel for rest and overnight stay.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, and Dinner Included

Accommodation: Hotel in Kyirong

Activity: Light exploration and rest

DAY 4 DRIVE TO SAGA 4,500m

High Plateau Journey: Early breakfast followed by departure for Saga (155km, approximately 3-4 hours). The drive offers spectacular views of the Tibetan plateau. En route, visit Ganesh Parbat and the stunning Lake Peiku Tso. Cross the bridge over the mighty Brahmaputra River (Yarlung Tsangpo) near Saga. Stop for lunch at a roadside restaurant. Arrive in Saga town, check into your hotel, and rest. Drink plenty of fluids and move slowly to aid altitude adjustment. Evening free for rest and acclimatization.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, and Dinner Included

Accommodation: Hotel in Saga

Drive Duration: 3-4 hours

DAY 5 SAGA TO LAKE MANSAROVAR 4,650m

Sacred Arrival: Early departure for the holy Lake Mansarovar (450km, approximately 8-9 hours). This long drive takes you across the vast Tibetan plateau with ever-changing landscapes. Cross the Mayum La Pass (5,150m) with traditional prayer flag offerings. Get your first glimpse of the sacred lake in the distance. Arrive at Lake Mansarovar guest house by late afternoon. Witness the stunning sunset over the pristine blue waters with Mount Kailash visible in the distance. Check into your basic guest house, rest, and prepare for tomorrow's spiritual activities.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, and Dinner Included

Accommodation: Guest House near Mansarovar

Drive Duration: 8-9 hours

DAY 6 LAKE MANSAROVAR TO DARCHEN 4,700m

Spiritual Preparation: Wake early for sunrise meditation and prayers at Lake Mansarovar. Perform puja/havan rituals at the lake shore - considered deeply auspicious in Hinduism and Buddhism. Take the holy dip if weather and health permit. After lunch, drive to Darchen (35km, approximately 1.5 hours), the base camp for Kailash Parikrama. Check into your hotel, then explore Darchen's small market in the late afternoon. Attend detailed briefing about the upcoming 3-day Kailash Parikrama. Prepare your backpack with essentials for the trek.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, and Dinner Included

Accommodation: Hotel in Darchen

Drive Duration: 1.5 hours

Note: Non-trekking participants will stay in Darchen with arranged accommodation and meals

DAY 7 YAMA DWAR TO DERAPUK 5,050m

Begin Parikrama: Drive to Yama Dwar, the "Gateway of the God of Death" and starting point of Kailash Parikrama. This spot offers your first great view of Mount Kailash's northern face. Crossing Yama Dwar symbolizes leaving the cycle of life and death and beginning the spiritual journey toward liberation. Trek 10-12km through stunning landscapes of rocky cliffs and waterfalls along the Lha-chu valley. The moderate trek takes 5-6 hours to reach Derapuk. Check into basic guest house with spectacular views of Kailash's north face - considered highly auspicious.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, and Dinner Included

Accommodation: Guest House at Derapuk

Trek Duration: 5-6 hours

Trek Distance: 10-12km

DAY 8 VIA DOLMA LA PASS TO ZUTHULPUK 5,600m (Dolma La)

Most Challenging Day: Early start for the trek to Dolma La Pass (5,600m), the highest point of Kailash Parikrama. The steep ascent tests physical endurance and spiritual determination. At the pass, offer prayers at Dolma La stone and tie prayer flags for blessings. Enjoy spectacular views of Mount Kailash's northeastern face, Nandi Parbat, and Gauri Kunda. The descent to Zuthulpuk (4,800m) is equally challenging. Arrive at Zuthulpuk ("miracle cave") associated with Milarepa by late afternoon. Check into basic guest house for well-deserved rest.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, and Dinner Included

Accommodation: Guest House at Zuthulpuk

Trek Duration: 8-9 hours

Trek Distance: 18-20km

Note: Personal riding horses available for rent

DAY 9 COMPLETE PARIKRAMA & RETURN TO SAGA 4,500m

Complete Sacred Circle: Final 8km trek completes the Kailash Parikrama, returning to Darchen (approximately 3-4 hours). Reunite with non-trekking group members. Feel profound sense of spiritual accomplishment. After quick celebrations, begin long drive back to Saga (485km, approximately 9-10 hours). Packed lunch en route. The return journey allows reflection on the transformative experience. Arrive in Saga by evening, check into hotel, and enjoy celebration dinner with fellow pilgrims. Share experiences and celebrate successful completion of the sacred journey.

Meals: Breakfast, Packed Lunch, and Dinner Included

Accommodation: Hotel in Saga

Trek Duration: 3-4 hours

Drive Duration: 9-10 hours

DAY 10 SAGA TO KYIRONG 2,850m

Return Journey: After breakfast, drive from Saga back to Kyirong (155km, approximately 3-4 hours). Descending to lower altitude brings relief after days at high elevation. The familiar landscape now holds new meaning after your spiritual journey. Arrive in Kyirong by early afternoon, check into hotel, and enjoy rest. Optional exploration of Kyirong markets for last-minute Tibetan souvenirs. Evening free for personal reflection and packing for tomorrow's border crossing. Enjoy improved sleep quality at lower altitude.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, and Dinner Included

Accommodation: Hotel in Kyirong

Drive Duration: 3-4 hours

DAY 11 KYIRONG TO KATHMANDU 1,400m

Return to Nepal: Early breakfast followed by drive to Rasuwagadhi border (40km, approximately 1 hour). Complete China exit formalities with assistance from Tibetan guide. Cross friendship bridge back into Nepal. Complete Nepal immigration procedures at Timure. Board Nepalese vehicles for drive back to Kathmandu (152km, approximately 7-8 hours). Stop for lunch en route. Arrive in Kathmandu by late afternoon. Receive certificate of completion commemorating your sacred journey. Transfer to hotel for farewell dinner and final night in Kathmandu.

Meals: Breakfast and Lunch Included

Accommodation: Hotel in Kathmandu

Drive Duration: 7-8 hours

DAY 12 DEPARTURE FROM KATHMANDU

Journey Completion: After breakfast, check out from hotel. Our representatives will assist with any onward travel arrangements. Depart with blessed memories, spiritual fulfillment, and the divine blessings of Mount Kailash and Lake Mansarovar. Carry with you the peace, enlightenment, and transformative experiences gained from this sacred pilgrimage. The journey may end, but the spiritual benefits continue throughout life.

Meals: Breakfast Included

Om Namah Shivaya! May Lord Shiva's blessings guide your path always!

Tour Concludes.

5-Day Detailed Itinerary

DAY 1 KATHMANDU TO ILAM BAZAAR 12-13 Hours Drive

Journey to Eastern Hills: Early morning departure from Kathmandu (6:00 AM) for the scenic drive to Ilam. The approximately 12-13 hour journey takes you through beautiful landscapes of eastern Nepal. You'll pass through Dhulikhel, Dharan, and then climb into the hills towards Ilam. Notice the changing vegetation from tropical to temperate as you gain altitude. Arrive at Ilam Bazaar by evening. Check into your hotel and enjoy your first experience of Ilam's cool, pleasant climate. Ilam Bazaar features colonial-style architecture and a charming atmosphere.

Meals: Lunch, Dinner Included

Accommodation: Hotel in Ilam Bazaar

Travel Duration: 12-13 hours

Highlights: Scenic mountain roads, changing landscapes, arrival in Ilam

DAY 2 ILAM BAZAAR TO ANTU DANDA TO MAI POKHARI 2,438m

Sunrise and Sacred Lake: Early morning drive (1.5 hours) to Antu Danda, famous for spectacular sunrise views over the Himalayas. On clear days, you can see Mount Kanchenjunga (world's third highest peak) and other Himalayan ranges. After enjoying sunrise and photography, continue to Mai Pokhari (2,438m altitude). Mai Pokhari is a sacred lake surrounded by rhododendron forests, considered holy by both Hindus and Buddhists. Explore the area, visit the temple, and enjoy the serene natural surroundings. Return to your hotel in Mai Pokhari area for overnight stay.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, and Dinner Included

Accommodation: Hotel in Mai Pokhari area

Travel Duration: 3-4 hours total driving

Highlights: Antu Danda sunrise, Mai Pokhari lake, Himalayan views

DAY 3 MAI POKHARI TO PASHUPATINAGAR TO FIKKAL Border Exploration

Border Town and Local Culture: After breakfast, drive to Pashupatinagar, a town on the Nepal-India border. Observe the cross-border activities and visit local markets where you can find goods from both countries. Continue to Fikkal, another important town in Ilam district. Explore Fikkal's local market and enjoy authentic local food for lunch. Fikkal offers insights into daily life in eastern Nepal's hill towns. You'll have time to interact with locals and learn about their traditions. Overnight stay at a hotel in Fikkal, enjoying the quiet, cool evening atmosphere.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, and Dinner Included

Accommodation: Hotel in Fikkal

Travel Duration: 2-3 hours driving

Highlights: Border experience, local markets, authentic Nepali cuisine

DAY 4 FIKKAL TO KANYAM TO ITAHARI Tea Garden Experience

Tea Gardens and City Life: Morning drive to Kanyam, famous for its beautiful tea gardens. Walk through the lush green tea plantations, learn about tea cultivation and processing. You may visit a tea factory if operational (subject to availability). Enjoy fresh Ilam tea tasting experience. After exploring Kanyam, begin drive to Itahari in the Terai region. Notice the dramatic change in climate and landscape as you descend from hills to plains. Arrive in Itahari by late afternoon. Explore Itahari Bazaar, a bustling commercial center in eastern Nepal. Overnight stay at hotel in Itahari.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, and Dinner Included

Accommodation: Hotel in Itahari

Travel Duration: 5-6 hours driving

Highlights: Kanyam tea gardens, tea tasting, Itahari city experience

DAY 5 ITAHARI TO KATHMANDU DRIVE Return Journey

Return to Capital: After breakfast at hotel, begin drive back to Kathmandu (approximately 6-7 hours). The route follows the East-West Highway with views of the Terai plains and distant hills. Stop for lunch en route at a local restaurant. Reflect on your wonderful experiences in Ilam - the tea gardens, mountain views, local culture, and peaceful atmosphere. Arrive in Kathmandu by late afternoon. Tour concludes with drop-off at your hotel or specified location in Kathmandu. Carry memories of Nepal's beautiful eastern hills and perhaps some Ilam tea as souvenirs.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch Included

Accommodation: Not Included (Tour Ends)

Travel Duration: 6-7 hours

Arrival: Kathmandu by late afternoon

Cultural Insights

Local Communities

Ilam is home to diverse ethnic groups including Limbu, Rai, Sherpa, and Brahmin/Chhetri communities. Each has unique traditions, festivals, and lifestyles. The Limbu community has particularly rich cultural heritage with traditional dance (Chyabrung) and music.

Tea Economy

Tea is the economic backbone of Ilam. The region produces orthodox tea, CTC tea, and green tea. Many families have worked in tea cultivation for generations. You'll see both large estates and smallholder tea gardens during your visit.

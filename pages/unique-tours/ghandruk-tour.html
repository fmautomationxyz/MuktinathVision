6-Day Ghandruk Cultural Trek

Discover the heart of Gurung culture with our 6-day Ghandruk Trek by Honest Travel Tours. Ghandruk (1,940m) is one of the largest and most beautiful Gurung villages in Nepal, offering spectacular views of Annapurna South, Hiunchuli, and Machhapuchhre (Fishtail Mountain). This trek combines moderate hiking with rich cultural immersion in the Annapurna region.

Mountain Views

From Ghandruk village, enjoy breathtaking views of Annapurna South (7,219m), Hiunchuli (6,441m), Gangapurna (7,455m), and the iconic Machhapuchhre (6,993m). The sunrise over these peaks is particularly spectacular. The village sits on a ridge facing north towards the mountains, offering unobstructed panoramic views.

Perfect for Cultural Enthusiasts & Nature Lovers

Our Ghandruk Trek is ideal for travelers seeking authentic cultural experiences without committing to strenuous high-altitude trekking. You'll interact with Gurung community members, visit local museums, taste traditional cuisine, and learn about the rich history of the Gurkha warriors. The trek offers comfortable accommodations and gentle hiking trails suitable for all ages.

6-Day Detailed Itinerary

DAY 1 ARRIVAL AT KATHMANDU Welcome to Nepal

Welcome to Kathmandu: Arrive at Tribhuvan International Airport where our representative will welcome you and transfer to your hotel. After check-in and refreshment, we'll have a short briefing about the trek. The rest of the day is free for you to explore Thamel, the tourist hub of Kathmandu. You can do last-minute shopping for trekking gear or simply relax at the hotel. In the evening, enjoy welcome dinner at a traditional Nepali restaurant with cultural program.

Meals: Dinner Included

Accommodation: Hotel in Kathmandu

Altitude: 1,400m

Highlights: Cultural welcome dinner, Thamel exploration

DAY 2 EXPLORE THE CULTURAL CITY OF KATHMANDU Sightseeing Day

Kathmandu Heritage Tour: After breakfast, embark on a full-day guided tour of Kathmandu's UNESCO World Heritage Sites. Visit Swayambhunath (Monkey Temple) for panoramic city views, then proceed to Patan Durbar Square to admire ancient Newari architecture. After lunch, explore Pashupatinath Temple, one of the most sacred Hindu temples, and Boudhanath Stupa, the largest stupa in Nepal. Evening free for final trek preparation. Optional briefing on trekking essentials and packing check.

Meals: Breakfast Included

Accommodation: Hotel in Kathmandu

Sites Covered: 4 UNESCO World Heritage Sites

Highlights: Ancient architecture, religious diversity

DAY 3 FLIGHT FROM KATHMANDU TO POKHARA, TREK TO POTHANA 1,860m / 6,102 feet

To the Mountains: Early morning flight to Pokhara (25 minutes) with spectacular Himalayan views on clear days. Upon arrival, drive to Kande (1 hour) - our trek starting point. Begin trek through beautiful rhododendron forests to Australian Camp (2 hours). Enjoy lunch with panoramic mountain views. After lunch, continue trek to Pothana (1.5 hours) through traditional villages. Check into teahouse and enjoy sunset views over Annapurna range. Evening briefing about upcoming trek days.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner Included

Accommodation: Teahouse in Pothana

Trek Duration: 3.5 hours

Altitude Gain: +460m from Kande

DAY 4 TREK FROM POTHANA TO GHANDRUK 1,940m / 6,365 feet

To Gurung Village: Early morning wake-up for sunrise views. After breakfast, descend through oak and rhododendron forests to Tolka village. Cross Modi Khola river and begin gradual ascent to Landruk village (1,640m). Enjoy lunch with views of Annapurna South. After lunch, continue trek along stone-paved trail to Ghandruk (2 hours). Arrive at Ghandruk - one of Nepal's largest and most beautiful Gurung villages. Check into traditional teahouse. Visit Gurung Museum and traditional houses. Optional cultural dance performance in evening.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner Included

Accommodation: Traditional teahouse in Ghandruk

Trek Duration: 5-6 hours

Highlights: Gurung culture, mountain views

DAY 5 TREK FROM GHANDRUK TO NAYAPUL, FLIGHT TO KATHMANDU Return Journey

Morning in Ghandruk & Return: Early morning sunrise viewing over Annapurna range - the highlight of the trek. After breakfast, explore Ghandruk village more deeply - visit local school, traditional houses, and interact with villagers. Begin descent to Nayapul (3-4 hours) via beautiful terraced fields and traditional villages. Drive to Pokhara airport (1.5 hours). Fly back to Kathmandu (25 minutes). Transfer to hotel. Evening farewell dinner celebrating successful trek completion. Share experiences and photos with group.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Farewell Dinner

Accommodation: Hotel in Kathmandu

Trek Duration: 3-4 hours descent

Flight: Pokhara to Kathmandu (25 minutes)

DAY 6 DEPART KATHMANDU Final Departure

Farewell: Depending on your flight schedule, enjoy final breakfast in Kathmandu. Our representative will transfer you to Tribhuvan International Airport 3 hours before your scheduled flight. Bid farewell to Nepal with beautiful memories of Himalayan landscapes and warm Gurung hospitality. Tour officially concludes at airport. Optional: If you have late flight, day use room can be arranged (additional cost) or we can arrange additional sightseeing/shopping.

Meals: Breakfast Included

Accommodation: Not Included (Tour Ends)

Airport Transfer: 3 hours before flight

Departure: According to flight schedule

Gurung Culture Insights

Traditional Lifestyle

Gurungs are known for their hospitality and rich cultural heritage. They live in stone houses with slate roofs, practice agriculture and animal husbandry. Many Gurungs serve in the British and Indian Gurkha regiments. Their traditional dress includes ghurung bangles and distinct woolen jackets.

Ghandruk Gurung Museum

The museum in Ghandruk showcases Gurung history, culture, and traditions. Displays include traditional clothing, agricultural tools, weapons, household items, and photographs of Gurkha soldiers. Entry fee supports local community development. Guided tours available.

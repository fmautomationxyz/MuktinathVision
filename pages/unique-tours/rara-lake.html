7-Day Driving Itinerary

DAY 1 KATHMANDU TO KOHALPUR 550km Drive

Long Drive West: Early morning departure from Kathmandu for approximately 12-14 hour drive to Kohalpur in Nepal's western Terai region. The journey follows the Prithvi Highway along the Trisuli River, then turns west on the Mahendra Highway. Enjoy changing landscapes from Kathmandu Valley to lower Terai plains. Arrive in Kohalpur by evening, check into hotel, and overnight stay.

Meals: Lunch, Dinner Included

Accommodation: Hotel in Kohalpur

Travel Duration: 12-14 hours

Road Condition: Mostly paved highway

DAY 2 KOHALPUR TO MANGMA (KALIKOT) 2,000m+

Into the Mountains: After breakfast, begin ascent into the hills of Karnali province. The drive becomes more challenging with winding mountain roads offering spectacular views. Pass through remote villages and witness traditional lifestyles. The route takes you through increasingly dramatic landscapes as you climb towards Kalikot district. Arrive at Mangma by evening, a key transit point for Rara Lake visits.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, and Dinner Included

Accommodation: Hotel/Lodge in Mangma

Travel Duration: 8-10 hours

Road Condition: Mountain roads, some rough sections

DAY 3 MANGMA TO RARA LAKE 2,990m

Reaching the Lake: Final drive to Rara Lake through Rara National Park. The forested route offers chances to spot wildlife including musk deer, Himalayan black bear, and various bird species. As you approach, catch first breathtaking glimpses of Nepal's largest lake. Arrive at Rara Lake by afternoon. Settle into accommodation, then take an evening walk along the lake shore to witness stunning sunset reflections on the crystal-clear water.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, and Dinner Included

Accommodation: Lodge at Rara Lake

Travel Duration: 4-6 hours

Road Condition: Rough mountain tracks, forest roads

DAY 4 EXPLORATION DAY AT RARA LAKE Exploration Day

Full Day at the Lake: Complete day to explore the beauty of Rara Lake and surrounding Rara National Park. Options include: hiking to viewpoints for panoramic lake views, walking the 13km circumference trail around the lake, bird watching (over 200 species recorded), visiting the Rara National Park headquarters, or simply relaxing by the pristine waters. The lake changes colors throughout the day from deep blue to emerald green. Packed lunch provided for your exploration.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, and Dinner Included

Activities: Hiking, Photography, Bird Watching

Accommodation: Lodge at Rara Lake

DAY 5 DRIVE BACK TO MANGMA Return Journey

Return from Paradise: After breakfast and final moments at Rara Lake, begin return journey. The drive back offers different perspectives of the landscapes you passed earlier. Stop at viewpoints for final photos of the Himalayas. Arrive back in Mangma by evening. This is a good opportunity to interact with locals and learn about life in remote Karnali region.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, and Dinner Included

Accommodation: Hotel/Lodge in Mangma

Travel Duration: 4-6 hours

DAY 6 DRIVE BACK TO KOHALPUR Descent to Plains

Return to Terai: Descend from the mountains back to the Terai plains. The winding road down offers last views of the Himalayan foothills. Arrive in Kohalpur by late afternoon. Evening free to relax, maybe explore the local market, or simply rest after the mountain journey.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, and Dinner Included

Accommodation: Hotel in Kohalpur

Travel Duration: 8-10 hours

DAY 7 DRIVE BACK TO KATHMANDU Return to Capital

Final Drive: After breakfast, begin long drive back to Kathmandu following the East-West Highway. Stop for lunch en route. Reflect on the incredible journey to one of Nepal's most remote and beautiful destinations. Arrive in Kathmandu by evening. Tour concludes with drop-off at your hotel or airport.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch Included

Accommodation: Not Included (Tour Ends)

Travel Duration: 12-14 hours

Arrival: Kathmandu by evening

15-Day Luxury Itinerary

DAY 1 ARRIVAL IN KATHMANDU 1,300m

Luxury Welcome: Arrive at Tribhuvan International Airport where our representative will greet you with traditional welcome and luxury vehicle transfer. Check into your 5-star hotel in Kathmandu. After settling in, attend a comprehensive pre-trek briefing with your guide over refreshments. Receive your premium trekking equipment and permits. Evening free to relax at the hotel or explore the vibrant Thamel area. Overnight in luxury accommodation with exceptional service.

Meals: None (Dinner not included)

Accommodation: 5-Star Hotel in Kathmandu

Activities: Arrival, briefing, equipment check

DAY 2 KATHMANDU VALLEY SIGHTSEEING TOUR

Cultural Immersion: After breakfast at your hotel, embark on a guided sightseeing tour of Kathmandu's UNESCO World Heritage Sites. Visit the sacred Hindu temple of Pashupatinath, the magnificent Buddhist stupa of Boudhanath, and the ancient royal palace of Patan Durbar Square. Learn about Nepal's rich cultural heritage from your expert guide. Enjoy lunch at a premium restaurant. Afternoon free for last-minute shopping or relaxation at your hotel. Evening briefing about the upcoming trek with your guide. Overnight in luxury accommodation.

Meals: Breakfast

Accommodation: 5-Star Hotel in Kathmandu

Activities: Cultural sightseeing, preparation

DAY 3 FLIGHT TO LUKLA & TREK TO PHAKDING 2,660m

Adventure Begins: Early morning transfer to airport for scenic mountain flight to Lukla (2,840m). Enjoy breakfast at the airport lounge before your flight. The 35-minute flight offers spectacular Himalayan views. Arrive at Tenzing-Hillary Airport, meet your guide and porter team, and begin trekking. Descend to the Dudh Koshi River, crossing several suspension bridges adorned with prayer flags. Arrive at Phakding village (2,660m) and check into one of the best lodges in the area. Enjoy a welcome dinner and briefing about the days ahead.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Accommodation: Luxury Lodge in Phakding

Trek Duration: 3-4 hours

Flight: 35 minutes

DAY 4 ENTER SAGARMATHA PARK & TREK TO NAMCHE 3,440m

Into the Khumbu: Morning trek with entry into Sagarmatha National Park, a UNESCO World Heritage Site. Follow the Dudh Koshi River, crossing the impressive Hillary Suspension Bridge. The trail gradually ascends through pine forests. After about 2 hours, catch your first glimpse of Everest peeking behind Lhotse-Nuptse ridge. The steep climb to Namche begins - take it slow and steady with your guide's support. Arrive at Namche Bazaar (3,440m), the bustling Sherpa capital. Check into the best available lodge with stunning mountain views and comfortable facilities.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Accommodation: Luxury Lodge in Namche Bazaar

Trek Duration: 6-7 hours

Altitude Gain: 780m

DAY 5 ACCLIMATIZATION AT EVEREST VIEW HOTEL

Altitude Adjustment: Essential rest day for acclimatization. Morning hike to Everest View Hotel (3,880m) for spectacular panoramic views of Everest, Lhotse, Nuptse, Ama Dablam, and other giants. Enjoy tea or coffee at the hotel while taking in the breathtaking scenery. Visit the Sherpa Museum to learn about local culture and mountaineering history. Afternoon free to explore Namche's market, visit local bakeries for fresh pastries, or relax at your lodge. Evening health check with your guide. "Climb high, sleep low" principle in practice with maximum comfort.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Accommodation: Luxury Lodge in Namche Bazaar

Activities: Acclimatization hike, cultural visit

DAY 6 TREK TO DEBUCHE 3,700m

Spiritual Journey: Spectacular trekking day with constant mountain views. Descend to the Dudh Koshi River, then gradual climb through beautiful rhododendron forests. Pass through Tengboche with its famous monastery, then continue to the peaceful village of Debuche (3,700m). Check into a comfortable lodge with views of Ama Dablam. If timing permits, visit the nunnery at Debuche. The quieter atmosphere here provides a perfect setting for relaxation and contemplation. Enjoy a delicious dinner prepared by lodge chefs.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Accommodation: Luxury Lodge in Debuche

Trek Duration: 6-7 hours

Altitude Gain: 260m

DAY 7 TREK TO DINGBOCHE 4,400m

High Altitude Landscape: Continue through alpine landscape with magnificent views of Ama Dablam. Pass through Pangboche village with its ancient monastery. Notice the change in vegetation as you enter higher altitude zone. Arrive at Dingboche (4,400m), a picturesque village surrounded by stone walls protecting crops from cold winds. Check into one of the best lodges in Dingboche with comfortable facilities. Afternoon free for rest and acclimatization. Drink plenty of fluids and enjoy the seasonal fruits provided.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Accommodation: Luxury Lodge in Dingboche

Trek Duration: 6-7 hours

Altitude Gain: 700m

DAY 8 ACCLIMATIZATION AT NAGARSHAN HILL

Second Acclimatization: Another crucial rest day for altitude adjustment. Morning hike to Nagarshan Hill (5,100m) or optional Chhukung Valley hike for spectacular views of Lhotse, Makalu, and Island Peak. This follows the "climb high, sleep low" principle essential for proper acclimatization. Return to Dingboche for lunch. Afternoon free for rest, reading, or exploring the village. This extra day significantly improves your body's adaptation to altitude, making the climb to Base Camp safer and more comfortable. Evening briefing about the upcoming days.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Accommodation: Luxury Lodge in Dingboche

Activities: Acclimatization hike to 5,100m

DAY 9 TREK TO LOBUCHE 4,970m

High Altitude Trekking: Gradual climb to Duglha (4,620m), then steep ascent to memorials for climbers who lost their lives on Everest. Take time to pay respects at these poignant memorials. The trail continues along the lateral moraine of Khumbu Glacier with spectacular views of Nuptse. Arrive at Lobuche (4,970m) and check into the best available lodge. Notice the thinner air and colder temperatures. Rest of the day for acclimatization - short walk to nearby ridges for views. Early dinner and rest, as tomorrow is the big day to Base Camp.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Accommodation: Luxury Lodge in Lobuche

Trek Duration: 6-7 hours

Altitude Gain: 570m

DAY 10 EVEREST BASE CAMP & GORAKSHEP 5,364m (EBC)

Dream Achievement: Early start for the highlight of the trek. Trek along the Khumbu Glacier moraine to Gorakshep (5,180m), the last settlement before Base Camp. Check into your lodge and have lunch. Then continue to Everest Base Camp (5,364m). The final section involves careful walking on rocky moraine. Arrive at Base Camp - a moment of pure achievement! During climbing season (April-May), see expedition tents. Marvel at the Khumbu Icefall. Take photos, celebrate with your team, then return to Gorakshep for overnight. Rest well after this big day.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Accommodation: Best Available Lodge in Gorakshep

Trek Duration: 7-9 hours

Highlight: Everest Base Camp at 5,364m

DAY 11 KALA PATTHAR & DESCEND TO PHERICHE 5,545m (Kala Patthar)

Best Everest Views: Pre-dawn start for Kala Patthar (5,545m) to witness sunrise over Everest. The steep climb is challenging but rewarded with the trek's most spectacular panorama. Watch the sun illuminate Everest's summit in golden light. Descend to Gorakshep for breakfast, then begin descent to lower altitude. Trek down to Pheriche (4,200m), noticing how much easier breathing becomes. Check into a comfortable lodge in Pheriche. Celebrate your achievements with your team over dinner. The descent brings welcome relief from high altitude.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Accommodation: Luxury Lodge in Pheriche

Trek Duration: 8-9 hours

Descent: 1,345m from Kala Patthar

DAY 12 TREK TO NAMCHE VIA TENGBOCHE

Return Journey: Continue descent through familiar terrain. Pass through Tengboche, stopping for lunch and possibly visiting the monastery. The trail feels much easier going downhill. Enjoy different perspectives of the mountains you've come to know. Cross suspension bridges and pass through forests that now feel lush and oxygen-rich. Arrive back at Namche Bazaar - returning to relative luxury after the high mountains. Check into your comfortable lodge. Enjoy hot showers and celebrate completion of the high-altitude section.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Accommodation: Luxury Lodge in Namche Bazaar

Trek Duration: 6-7 hours

Descent: 1,240m

DAY 13 TREK TO LUKLA 2,840m

Final Trekking Day: Last day of trekking retraces your steps along the Dudh Koshi River. The descent is mostly downhill with a few uphill sections. Cross the Hillary Bridge one last time. Arrive at Lukla (2,840m) by afternoon - marking the end of your trekking adventure. Check into the best available lodge. Evening celebration dinner with your guide and porter team. Traditional Nepali dance and music performance (if available). Receive your adventure certificate. Pack for tomorrow's flight and enjoy your last night in the mountains.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Accommodation: Luxury Lodge in Lukla

Trek Duration: 6-7 hours

Descent: 600m from Namche

DAY 14 FLIGHT TO KATHMANDU & FAREWELL DINNER

Return to Kathmandu: Early morning flight back to Kathmandu (weather permitting). Bid farewell to your mountain crew at Lukla airport. The 35-minute flight offers final aerial views of the Himalayas. Arrive in Kathmandu and transfer to your 5-star hotel. Rest of the day free for relaxation, souvenir shopping, spa treatments, or exploring Kathmandu. Evening farewell dinner at a premium traditional Nepali restaurant with cultural show. Celebrate your incredible achievement with your fellow trekkers and guides. Overnight in luxury accommodation.

Meals: Breakfast, Farewell Dinner

Accommodation: 5-Star Hotel in Kathmandu

Flight: 35 minutes

Activities: Flight, free time, celebration dinner

DAY 15 DEPARTURE

Journey Completion: Depending on your flight schedule, enjoy final breakfast at your luxury hotel. Our representative will transfer you to Tribhuvan International Airport 3 hours before your flight. Depart with incredible memories, stunning photographs, and the satisfaction of having completed the Everest Luxury Trek in maximum comfort. Carry with you not just souvenirs, but experiences of Himalayan grandeur, Sherpa hospitality, and personal achievement that will stay with you forever.

Meals: Breakfast

Safe travels and hope to see you again in the Himalayas!

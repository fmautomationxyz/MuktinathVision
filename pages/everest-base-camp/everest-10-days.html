Express Journey to the Foot of the World's Highest Mountain

Fast-Track Everest Adventure

Why Choose Our 10-Day Express Itinerary?

Our 10-day express program is perfect for travelers with limited time but big ambitions. While shorter than the classic itinerary, we've carefully designed the schedule to include essential acclimatization days while maintaining a steady pace. This itinerary is ideal for experienced trekkers or those with good fitness levels who want to experience the Everest region without taking extended time off work. You'll still experience the magic of the Himalayas, Sherpa culture, and the ultimate achievement of reaching Everest Base Camp.

10-Day Detailed Itinerary

DAY 1 FLY TO LUKLA & TREK TO PHAKDING 2,645m

Adventure Begins: Early morning transfer to Kathmandu airport for scenic mountain flight to Lukla (2,846m). The 35-minute flight offers spectacular Himalayan views. Arrive at Tenzing-Hillary Airport, meet your guide and porter team. Begin trekking with a gentle descent to the Dudh Koshi River, crossing several suspension bridges adorned with prayer flags. The trail leads through Sherpa villages with picturesque mountain views. Arrive at Phakding village (2,645m) in about 3-4 hours. Check into teahouse, explore the village, and rest for tomorrow's climb to Namche.

Meals: Lunch, Dinner

Accommodation: Teahouse in Phakding

Trek Duration: 3-4 hours

Flight: 35 minutes

DAY 2 TREK TO NAMCHE BAZAAR 3,440m

Entering the Khumbu: Follow the Dudh Koshi River, crossing the impressive Hillary Suspension Bridge. The trail gradually ascends through pine forests. After about 2 hours, catch your first glimpse of Everest peeking behind Lhotse-Nuptse ridge. The steep climb to Namche begins here - take it slow and steady. Arrive at Namche Bazaar (3,440m), the bustling Sherpa capital and trading hub. Check into teahouse with stunning mountain views. Explore the vibrant town with its shops, bakeries, and cafes. Evening briefing on altitude awareness.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Accommodation: Teahouse in Namche Bazaar

Trek Duration: 6-7 hours

Altitude Gain: 795m

DAY 3 TREK TO TENGBOCHE 3,875m

Spiritual Heart: Spectacular trekking day with constant mountain views. The trail offers magnificent views of Everest, Lhotse, Nuptse, and Ama Dablam. Descend to the Dudh Koshi River, then gradual climb through rhododendron forests. Arrive at Tengboche Monastery (3,875m), the largest in the Khumbu region. Attend evening prayers with chanting monks (if available). The monastery offers stunning sunset views of Everest, Lhotse, and Ama Dablam. Stay in teahouse with arguably the best mountain views of the trek.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Accommodation: Teahouse in Tengboche

Trek Duration: 6-7 hours

Altitude Gain: 435m

DAY 4 TREK TO DINGBOCHE 4,420m

High Altitude Landscape: Descend through beautiful forest to Deboche, then cross Imja Khola river. Gradual climb to Pangboche village with views of Ama Dablam's imposing north face. Continue ascending through alpine landscape with fewer trees. Arrive at Dingboche (4,420m), a picturesque village surrounded by stone walls protecting crops from cold winds. Notice the change in vegetation as you enter higher altitude zone. Afternoon free for rest and acclimatization. Drink plenty of fluids and move slowly.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Accommodation: Teahouse in Dingboche

Trek Duration: 5-6 hours

Altitude Gain: 545m

DAY 5 TREK TO LOBUCHE 4,960m

High Altitude Trekking: Gradual climb to Duglha (4,620m), then steep ascent to memorials for climbers who lost their lives on Everest. Take time to pay respects at these poignant memorials. The trail continues along the lateral moraine of Khumbu Glacier with spectacular views of Nuptse. Arrive at Lobuche (4,960m), a small settlement with basic teahouses. Notice the thinner air and colder temperatures. Rest of the day for acclimatization - short walk to nearby ridges for views. Early dinner and rest, as tomorrow is the big day to Base Camp.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Accommodation: Teahouse in Lobuche

Trek Duration: 5-6 hours

Altitude Gain: 540m

DAY 6 EVEREST BASE CAMP & GORAKSHEP 5,180m

Dream Achievement: Early start for the highlight of the trek. Trek along the Khumbu Glacier moraine to Gorakshep (5,180m), the last settlement before Base Camp. After quick lunch, continue to Everest Base Camp (5,364m). The final section involves careful walking on rocky moraine. Arrive at Base Camp - a moment of pure achievement! During climbing season (April-May), see expedition tents. Marvel at the Khumbu Icefall, the most dangerous section of the Everest climb. Take photos, celebrate, then return to Gorakshep for overnight. Drink plenty and rest well after this big day.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Accommodation: Teahouse in Gorakshep

Trek Duration: 6-7 hours (3 hrs to Gorakshep + 3-4 hrs to EBC)

Altitude: Base Camp 5,364m

DAY 7 KALA PATTHAR & DESCEND TO PANGBOCHE 5,545m (Kala Patthar)

Best Everest Views: Pre-dawn start for Kala Patthar (5,545m) to witness sunrise over Everest. The steep climb is challenging but rewarded with the trek's most spectacular panorama. Watch the sun illuminate Everest's summit in golden light, with panoramic views of surrounding giants. Descend to Gorakshep for breakfast, then begin long descent to lower altitude. Pass through Lobuche and Duglha, continuing down to Pheriche valley. Arrive at Pangboche village (4,010m) by late afternoon. Notice how much easier breathing becomes at lower elevation. Celebrate your achievements with fellow trekkers.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Accommodation: Teahouse in Pangboche

Trek Duration: 7-8 hours

Descent: 1,535m from Kala Patthar

DAY 8 TREK TO NAMCHE BAZAAR 3,440m

Return Journey: Continue descent through familiar terrain. Pass through Tengboche, stopping for lunch. The trail feels much easier going downhill. Enjoy different perspectives of the mountains you've come to know. Cross suspension bridges and pass through forests that now feel lush and oxygen-rich. Arrive back at Namche Bazaar - it feels like returning to civilization after the high mountains. Enjoy hot showers (available at extra cost), visit bakeries for fresh treats, and celebrate completion of the high-altitude section. Evening free for relaxation and souvenir shopping.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Accommodation: Teahouse in Namche Bazaar

Trek Duration: 7-8 hours

Descent: 570m

DAY 9 TREK TO LUKLA 2,820m

Final Trekking Day: Last day of trekking retraces your steps along the Dudh Koshi River. The descent is mostly downhill with a few uphill sections. Cross the Hillary Bridge one last time. Arrive at Lukla (2,820m) by afternoon - a significant milestone marking the end of your trek. Check into teahouse, then enjoy free time. Evening celebration dinner with your guide and porter team. Traditional Nepali dance and music performance (if available). Receive certificates of achievement. Pack for tomorrow's flight and enjoy your last night in the mountains.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Accommodation: Teahouse in Lukla

Trek Duration: 7-8 hours

Descent: 620m

DAY 10 FLY TO KATHMANDU & EXPLORE

Return to Kathmandu: Early morning flight back to Kathmandu (weather permitting). Bid farewell to your mountain crew. The 35-minute flight offers final aerial views of the Himalayas. Transfer from Kathmandu airport. Rest of the day free for exploring Kathmandu's cultural sites, souvenir shopping, or relaxation. Optional visits to Swayambhunath (Monkey Temple), Boudhanath Stupa, or Patan Durbar Square. Evening farewell dinner at traditional Nepali restaurant with cultural show (optional). Overnight in Kathmandu (not included in package), enjoying comfortable beds and reflecting on your incredible Himalayan achievement.

Meals: Breakfast

Flight: 35 minutes

Activities: Flight, free time in Kathmandu

Safe travels and hope to see you again in the Himalayas!

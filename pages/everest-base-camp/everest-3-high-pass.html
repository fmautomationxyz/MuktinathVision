20 Days Everest 3 High Passes Trek

Ultimate Himalayan Adventure: Kongma La, Cho La & Renjo La Passes

The Ultimate Everest Circuit

The Everest 3 High Passes Trek is the most comprehensive and challenging adventure in the Khumbu region, combining the classic Everest Base Camp trek with three spectacular high mountain passes over 5,350m. This 20-day epic journey takes you through the heart of the Everest region, offering unparalleled panoramic views, remote valleys, glacial lakes, and a complete Himalayan experience that few trekkers ever achieve.

Complete Himalayan Experience

  • Three High Passes: Kongma La (5,535m), Cho La (5,420m), and Renjo La (5,367m)
  • Dual Base Camps: Visit both Everest Base Camp and the stunning Gokyo Lakes
  • 360-Degree Views: Panoramic vistas from Kala Patthar (5,545m) and Gokyo Ri (5,357m)
  • Remote Trails: Experience less-traveled routes away from the main trekking crowds
  • Complete Circuit: Circular route that avoids backtracking on the same trails
  • Ultimate Challenge: The most comprehensive trek in the Everest region

Trip Facts

  • Duration: 19 Nights / 20 Days
  • Starting Point: Kathmandu, Nepal
  • Maximum Altitude: 5,545m (Kala Patthar)
  • Highest Pass: Kongma La Pass (5,535m)
  • Trek Difficulty: Strenuous/Challenging
  • Best Seasons: April-May & September-November
  • Daily Trekking: 5-8 hours average
  • Group Size: 4-10 trekkers per group

Why Choose the 3 High Passes Trek?

This trek is designed for experienced trekkers seeking the ultimate Himalayan challenge. Unlike standard routes, the Three High Passes circuit offers a complete 360-degree tour of the Everest region. You'll experience the thrill of crossing three high mountain passes, visit both Everest Base Camp and the breathtaking Gokyo Lakes, and enjoy spectacular viewpoints that few trekkers ever see. The route minimizes backtracking and takes you through remote valleys and traditional Sherpa villages that remain untouched by mass tourism. This is not just a trek—it's an expedition that will test your limits and reward you with memories to last a lifetime.

20-Day Detailed Itinerary

DAY 1 ARRIVAL IN KATHMANDU 1,365m

Welcome to Nepal: Arrive at Tribhuvan International Airport where our representative will greet you with traditional welcome. Transfer to your hotel in the heart of Kathmandu. After check-in and refreshment, attend a comprehensive pre-trek briefing with your guide. Receive your trekking permits and final equipment check. Evening free to explore Thamel area or rest at your hotel. Overnight in Kathmandu with dinner on your own account.

Meals: None (Dinner not included)

Accommodation: Hotel in Kathmandu

Activities: Arrival, briefing, preparation

DAY 2 FLIGHT TO LUKLA & TREK TO PHAKDING 2,657m

Adventure Begins: Early morning transfer to airport for scenic mountain flight to Lukla (2,820m). The 35-minute flight offers spectacular Himalayan views. Arrive at Tenzing-Hillary Airport, meet your porter team, and begin trekking. Descend to the Dudh Koshi River, crossing several suspension bridges adorned with prayer flags. The gentle trail leads through Sherpa villages with picturesque mountain views. Arrive at Phakding village (2,657m) in about 3-4 hours. Check into teahouse, explore the village, and rest for tomorrow's climb to Namche.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Accommodation: Teahouse in Phakding

Trek Duration: 3-4 hours

Flight: 35 minutes

DAY 3 TREK TO NAMCHE BAZAAR 3,440m

Entering the Khumbu: Follow the Dudh Koshi River, crossing the impressive Hillary Suspension Bridge. The trail gradually ascends through pine forests. After about 2 hours, catch your first glimpse of Everest peeking behind Lhotse-Nuptse ridge. The steep climb to Namche begins here - take it slow and steady. Arrive at Namche Bazaar (3,440m), the bustling Sherpa capital and trading hub. Check into teahouse with stunning mountain views. Explore the vibrant town with its shops, bakeries, and cafes. Evening briefing on altitude awareness.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Accommodation: Teahouse in Namche Bazaar

Trek Duration: 6-7 hours

Altitude Gain: 783m

DAY 4 ACCLIMATIZATION IN NAMCHE

Altitude Adjustment: Essential rest day for acclimatization. Morning hike to Everest View Hotel (3,880m) for spectacular panoramic views of Everest, Lhotse, Nuptse, Ama Dablam, and other giants. Visit the Sherpa Museum to learn about local culture and mountaineering history. Optional visit to Sagarmatha National Park Visitor Center. Afternoon free to explore Namche's market, visit local bakeries, or simply rest. Important day for altitude adjustment - "climb high, sleep low" principle in practice. Evening health check with your guide.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Accommodation: Teahouse in Namche Bazaar

Activities: Acclimatization hike, cultural visit

DAY 5 TREK TO TENGBOCHE 3,867m

Spiritual Heart: Spectacular trekking day with constant mountain views. Descend to the Dudh Koshi River, then gradual climb through rhododendron forests. Stop for lunch at Phunki Tenga (3,250m). Afternoon ascent to Tengboche - the trail offers magnificent views of Ama Dablam. Arrive at Tengboche Monastery (Thyangboche), the largest in the Khumbu region. Attend evening prayers with chanting monks (if available). The monastery offers stunning sunset views of Everest, Lhotse, and Ama Dablam. Stay in teahouse with arguably the best mountain views of the trek.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Accommodation: Teahouse in Tengboche

Trek Duration: 6-7 hours

Altitude Gain: 427m

DAY 6 TREK TO DINGBOCHE 4,420m

High Altitude Landscape: Descend through beautiful forest to Deboche, then cross Imja Khola river. Gradual climb to Pangboche village with views of Ama Dablam's imposing north face. Visit Pangboche Monastery, one of the oldest in the region. Continue ascending through alpine landscape with fewer trees. Arrive at Dingboche village (4,420m), a picturesque settlement surrounded by stone walls protecting crops from cold winds. Notice the change in vegetation as you enter higher altitude zone. Afternoon free for rest and acclimatization.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Accommodation: Teahouse in Dingboche

Trek Duration: 5-6 hours

Altitude Gain: 553m

DAY 7 ACCLIMATIZATION DAY (NAGARSHUNG HILL)

Second Acclimatization: Another crucial rest day for altitude adjustment. Morning hike to Nagarshung Hill (5,100m) for spectacular views of Lhotse, Makalu, Island Peak, and the surrounding peaks. This hike follows the "climb high, sleep low" principle, essential for proper acclimatization. Return to Dingboche for lunch. Afternoon free for rest, journal writing, or exploring the village. This extra day at 4,420m significantly improves your body's adaptation to altitude, making the upcoming high passes safer and more comfortable. Evening briefing about the upcoming passes.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Accommodation: Teahouse in Dingboche

Activities: Acclimatization hike to 5,100m

DAY 8 TREK TO CHHUKUNG 4,750m

Gateway to Kongma La: Short but steep trek to Chhukung village (4,750m), located at the base of Island Peak. The trail follows the Imja Valley with spectacular views of Island Peak (6,189m) and Lhotse's south face. Chhukung is a small settlement with basic teahouses, serving as the staging point for Island Peak climbs and the Kongma La Pass crossing. Afternoon acclimatization hike to Chhukung Ri (5,546m) for those feeling strong, or rest and prepare for tomorrow's challenging pass crossing. Early dinner and rest.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Accommodation: Teahouse in Chhukung

Trek Duration: 3-4 hours

Altitude Gain: 330m

DAY 9 KONGMA LA PASS TO LOBUCHE 5,535m (Pass)

First High Pass: Early start for the challenging crossing of Kongma La Pass (5,535m), the highest of the three passes. The steep ascent takes you over rocky moraine and glacial terrain. From the pass, enjoy breathtaking 360-degree views of the Khumbu Glacier, Nuptse, Lhotse, Makalu, and countless other peaks. The descent is steep and rocky down to the Khumbu Glacier. Cross the glacier (carefully following your guide) to reach Lobuche (4,970m). This is a long and demanding day - celebrate your achievement! Rest well after this challenging crossing.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Accommodation: Teahouse in Lobuche

Trek Duration: 8 hours

Challenge: High altitude pass crossing

DAY 10 EVEREST BASE CAMP & GORAKSHEP 5,364m (EBC)

Dream Achievement: Trek along the Khumbu Glacier moraine to Gorakshep (5,180m), the last settlement before Base Camp. After quick lunch, continue to Everest Base Camp (5,364m). The final section involves careful walking on rocky moraine. Arrive at Base Camp - a moment of pure achievement! During climbing season (April-May), see expedition tents. Marvel at the Khumbu Icefall, the most dangerous section of the Everest climb. Take photos, celebrate, then return to Gorakshep for overnight. Drink plenty and rest well after this big day.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Accommodation: Teahouse in Gorakshep

Trek Duration: 6-7 hours (3 hrs to Gorakshep + 3-4 hrs to EBC)

Highlight: Everest Base Camp at 5,364m

DAY 11 KALA PATTHAR & TREK TO DZONGLA 5,545m (Kala Patthar)

Best Everest Views: Pre-dawn start for Kala Patthar (5,545m) to witness sunrise over Everest. The steep climb is challenging but rewarded with the trek's most spectacular panorama. Watch the sun illuminate Everest's summit in golden light, with panoramic views of surrounding giants. Descend to Gorakshep for breakfast, then trek to Dzongla (4,830m). The trail descends through the Khumbu Valley, offering different perspectives of the mountains. Dzongla is a small, remote settlement with stunning views of Cholatse and Arakam Tse. Prepare for tomorrow's Cho La Pass.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Accommodation: Teahouse in Dzongla

Trek Duration: 5 hours

Views: Sunrise over Everest from Kala Patthar

DAY 12 CHO LA PASS TO THANGNA 5,420m (Pass)

Second High Pass: Early start for Cho La Pass (5,420m), the most famous of the three passes. The ascent involves crossing a small glacier - crampons may be required depending on conditions. The pass itself is marked by prayer flags and offers spectacular views of the Gokyo Valley and surrounding peaks. The descent is steep and rocky into the Gokyo Valley. Arrive at Thangna (4,750m), a small settlement near the Ngozumpa Glacier, the largest glacier in Nepal. Rest and recover from this challenging day. The scenery changes dramatically as you enter the Gokyo Valley.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Accommodation: Teahouse in Thangna

Trek Duration: 7-8 hours

Challenge: Glacier crossing and steep descent

DAY 13 TREK TO GOKYO LAKES 4,750m

Turquoise Paradise: Short trek to Gokyo village situated beside the stunning Gokyo Lakes. These six turquoise lakes are considered sacred by both Hindus and Buddhists. The largest, Dudh Pokhari, is particularly magnificent with Cho Oyu (8,188m) reflected in its waters. Afternoon free to explore the lakeshore, visit the small Gokyo village, or simply rest and enjoy the breathtaking scenery. The Gokyo Valley offers a different perspective of the Himalayas, with Cho Oyu dominating the northern horizon. Prepare for tomorrow's climb to Gokyo Ri.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Accommodation: Teahouse in Gokyo

Trek Duration: 3-4 hours

Scenery: Sacred turquoise lakes

DAY 14 GOKYO RI VIEWPOINT 5,357m

Panoramic Masterpiece: Early morning climb to Gokyo Ri (5,357m) for one of the best panoramic views in the Himalayas. From the summit, enjoy 360-degree views encompassing four 8,000-meter peaks: Everest, Lhotse, Makalu, and Cho Oyu. The view down to the Gokyo Lakes is spectacular. Many trekkers consider this viewpoint even better than Kala Patthar. Return to Gokyo for breakfast. Rest of the day free for relaxation, exploring other lakes, or optional hike to Fifth Lake and the Ngozumpa Glacier viewpoint. Evening to reflect on your Himalayan achievements.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Accommodation: Teahouse in Gokyo

Trek Duration: 3-4 hours (round trip)

Views: Four 8,000m peaks visible

DAY 15 RENJO LA PASS TO LUNGDEN 5,367m (Pass)

Third High Pass: Final pass crossing - Renjo La Pass (5,367m). The ascent is steady with spectacular views back to Gokyo Lakes and the surrounding peaks. From the pass, enjoy breathtaking views of Everest, Lhotse, and Makalu to the east. The descent into the Bhote Koshi Valley is long but beautiful, passing through remote landscapes with few other trekkers. Arrive at Lungden (4,600m), a small settlement in the remote Bhote Koshi Valley. This area feels truly isolated and offers a glimpse of traditional Tibetan-influenced culture. Celebrate completing all three high passes!

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Accommodation: Teahouse in Lungden

Trek Duration: 7-8 hours

Achievement: All three high passes completed

DAY 16 TREK TO THAME VILLAGE 3,800m

Cultural Descent: Descend the Bhote Koshi Valley to Thame village (3,800m), an important trading village on the ancient Tibet-Nepal trade route. Thame is known for its historic monastery and as the hometown of many famous Sherpa climbers, including Apa Sherpa who summited Everest 21 times. Visit Thame Monastery, one of the oldest in the region, perched dramatically on a hillside. The village offers insights into traditional Sherpa life away from the busier trekking routes. Enjoy the warmer temperatures and thicker air at this lower altitude.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Accommodation: Teahouse in Thame

Trek Duration: 5-6 hours

Altitude Loss: 800m descent

DAY 17 TREK TO NAMCHE BAZAAR

Return to Civilization: Trek from Thame to Namche Bazaar, completing the circular route. The trail follows the Bhote Koshi River downstream, passing through several small villages. Notice how the landscape becomes greener and more forested as you descend. Arrive back at Namche Bazaar - it feels like returning to civilization after the remote high passes. Enjoy hot showers (available at extra cost), visit bakeries for fresh treats, and celebrate completion of the entire Three High Passes circuit. Evening free for relaxation, souvenir shopping, and farewell dinner with your team.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Accommodation: Teahouse in Namche Bazaar

Trek Duration: 5-6 hours

Distance: Approximately 10km

DAY 18 TREK TO LUKLA 2,820m

Final Trekking Day: Last day of trekking retraces your steps along the Dudh Koshi River. The descent is mostly downhill with a few uphill sections. Cross the Hillary Bridge one last time. Arrive at Lukla (2,820m) by afternoon - a significant milestone marking the end of your epic 20-day adventure. Check into teahouse, then enjoy free time. Evening celebration dinner with your guide and porter team. Traditional Nepali dance and music performance (if available). Receive certificates of achievement for completing the Three High Passes Trek. Pack for tomorrow's flight.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Accommodation: Teahouse in Lukla

Trek Duration: 7-8 hours

Altitude Loss: 620m from Namche

DAY 19 FLY TO KATHMANDU

Return to Kathmandu: Early morning flight back to Kathmandu (weather permitting). Bid farewell to your mountain crew at Lukla airport. The 35-minute flight offers final aerial views of the Himalayas. Arrive in Kathmandu and transfer to your hotel. Rest of the day free for relaxation, souvenir shopping, or exploring Kathmandu's cultural sites. Optional visits to Swayambhunath (Monkey Temple), Boudhanath Stupa, or Patan Durbar Square. Evening farewell dinner at traditional Nepali restaurant with cultural show (optional). Overnight in Kathmandu, enjoying comfortable beds and reflecting on your incredible Himalayan achievement.

Meals: Breakfast

Accommodation: Hotel in Kathmandu

Flight: 35 minutes

Activities: Flight, free time in Kathmandu

DAY 20 FINAL DEPARTURE

Journey Completion: Depending on your flight schedule, enjoy final breakfast in Kathmandu. Our representative will transfer you to Tribhuvan International Airport 3 hours before your flight. Depart with incredible memories, stunning photographs, and the satisfaction of having completed one of the world's most challenging and rewarding treks. Carry with you not just souvenirs, but experiences of Himalayan grandeur, Sherpa hospitality, and personal achievement that will stay with you forever.

Meals: Breakfast

Safe travels and hope to see you again in the Himalayas!

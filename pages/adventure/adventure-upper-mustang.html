Upper Mustang 4WD Jeep Adventure Tour

Journey to Nepal's Last Forbidden Kingdom

Tour Overview

Embark on an epic 4WD jeep adventure through Nepal's mystical Upper Mustang region, often called the "Last Forbidden Kingdom." This 12-day journey takes you through ancient trade routes, Tibetan-influenced villages, dramatic desert landscapes, and to the remote walled city of Lo Manthang. Experience a unique blend of Tibetan Buddhist culture, stunning Himalayan scenery, and comfortable jeep travel through one of Nepal's most restricted and fascinating regions.

Detailed Itinerary

DAY 1 ARRIVAL IN KATHMANDU 1,400m

Welcome to Nepal: Arrive at Tribhuvan International Airport where our representative will welcome you with traditional greetings. Transfer to your hotel in Kathmandu. After check-in, attend a detailed tour briefing about your Upper Mustang jeep adventure. Rest and acclimate to the altitude. Evening at leisure.

Meals: Welcome Dinner Included

Accommodation: Hotel in Kathmandu

DAY 2 KATHMANDU FULL DAY SIGHTSEEING

Cultural Exploration: Full day exploring Kathmandu's UNESCO World Heritage Sites. Visit Swayambhunath Stupa (Monkey Temple) for panoramic city views. Explore Kathmandu Durbar Square with its ancient palaces and temples. After lunch, visit the sacred Pashupatinath Temple and Boudhanath Stupa, one of the largest spherical stupas in the world. Prepare for your Mustang adventure.

Meals: Breakfast Included

Accommodation: Hotel in Kathmandu

DAY 3 DRIVE TO BANDIPUR 1,030m

Scenic Mountain Drive: Early morning departure from Kathmandu. Drive through picturesque landscapes to Bandipur, a beautifully preserved Newari town. Enjoy stunning views of the Himalayas along the way. Explore Bandipur's medieval architecture, traditional houses, and vibrant culture. Evening stroll through the town's cobbled streets. Overnight in a heritage hotel.

Meals: Breakfast, Dinner Included

Accommodation: Heritage Hotel in Bandipur

Drive Duration: 5-6 hours

DAY 4 BANDIPUR SUNRISE & DRIVE TO TATOPANI 1,190m

Sunrise & Natural Springs: Early morning sunrise view over the Himalayas from Bandipur. After breakfast, drive to Tatopani ("Hot Water" in Nepali). Descend through deep river gorges and scenic landscapes. Arrive at Tatopani and relax in natural hot springs beside the Kali Gandaki River - perfect for soothing muscles after the journey. Overnight at a local lodge.

Meals: Breakfast, Dinner Included

Accommodation: Lodge in Tatopani

Drive Duration: 4-5 hours

DAY 5 DRIVE TO KAGBENI, ENROUTE SIGHTSEEING 2,800m

Gateway to Upper Mustang: Drive north along the Kali Gandaki River valley, the world's deepest gorge. Pass through dramatic landscapes transitioning to semi-arid desert. Visit Marpha village known for apple products. Continue to Kagbeni, the traditional gateway to Upper Mustang. Explore this ancient village with its medieval feel and Tibetan influence. Acclimatize to the higher altitude.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner Included

Accommodation: Lodge in Kagbeni

Drive Duration: 5-6 hours

DAY 6 MUKTINATH TEMPLE VISIT, DRIVE TO LO MANTHANG 3,800m

Sacred Site & Forbidden Kingdom: Morning visit to Muktinath Temple, one of Nepal's most sacred pilgrimage sites for both Hindus and Buddhists. See the 108 water spouts and eternal flame. Continue your jeep journey through spectacular desert landscapes with colorful rock formations. Arrive at the walled city of Lo Manthang, capital of the former Kingdom of Mustang. Check-in and rest.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner Included

Accommodation: Lodge in Lo Manthang

Drive Duration: 4-5 hours

DAY 7 LO MANTHANG EXPLORATION DAY

Ancient Kingdom Discovery: Full day to explore the fascinating walled city of Lo Manthang. Visit the Royal Palace, three major monasteries (Thubchen, Chodey, and Jampa), and ancient cave dwellings. Experience the unique Tibetan Buddhist culture preserved for centuries. Option to take a short hike to nearby villages or viewpoints for panoramic photography. Evening interaction with local community.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner Included

Accommodation: Lodge in Lo Manthang

DAY 8 DRIVE TO MARPHA 2,670m

Return Journey & Apple Capital: Begin return journey with a drive back to Marpha. Enjoy different perspectives of the dramatic landscapes. Arriving in Marpha, explore this charming village known as the "Apple Capital of Nepal." Sample local apple brandy, cider, and other apple products. Walk through narrow stone paved streets lined with whitewashed houses. Relax and reflect on your Mustang experience.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner Included

Accommodation: Lodge in Marpha

Drive Duration: 5-6 hours

DAY 9 DRIVE TO POKHARA, PHEWA LAKE KAYAKING 827m

Scenic Drive & Lake Adventure: Morning drive from Marpha to Pokhara through beautiful Himalayan scenery. Descend from the dry Tibetan plateau to the lush greenery of Pokhara valley. Arrive in Pokhara, check into your hotel. Afternoon kayaking on Phewa Lake with stunning views of the Annapurna range. Paddle to the Peace Pagoda for sunset views. Evening at leisure in Lakeside.

Meals: Breakfast Included

Accommodation: Hotel in Pokhara

Drive Duration: 6-7 hours

DAY 10 POKHARA FULL DAY SIGHTSEEING

Adventure Capital Exploration: Full day exploring Pokhara's attractions. Visit Davis Falls, Gupteshwor Cave, and the International Mountain Museum. Take a boat ride on Phewa Lake (if time permits). Drive to Sarangkot viewpoint for panoramic Himalayan vistas (optional sunrise/sunset). Explore Lakeside area for shopping and dining. Relax after your adventurous Mustang journey.

Meals: Breakfast Included

Accommodation: Hotel in Pokhara

DAY 11 FLY TO KATHMANDU, CULTURAL DINNER

Scenic Flight & Celebration: Morning transfer to Pokhara Airport for flight to Kathmandu (25 minutes) with breathtaking mountain views. Upon arrival, transfer to hotel. Free time for last-minute shopping or optional sightseeing. Evening farewell Nepali cultural dinner with traditional music, dance performances, and authentic cuisine. Celebrate your successful Upper Mustang adventure.

Meals: Breakfast, Farewell Dinner Included

Accommodation: Hotel in Kathmandu

DAY 12 DEPARTURE FROM NEPAL

Journey Ends: After breakfast, transfer to Tribhuvan International Airport for your onward flight. Our representative will assist with check-in procedures. Depart with unforgettable memories of Nepal's Last Forbidden Kingdom, stunning landscapes, and rich cultural experiences.

Meals: Breakfast Included

Thank you for choosing Honest Travel Tours. Safe travels and hope to welcome you back to Nepal!

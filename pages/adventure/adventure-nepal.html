Detailed Itinerary

DAY 1 ARRIVAL IN KATHMANDU 1,400m

Welcome to Nepal: Upon arrival at Tribhuvan International Airport, our representative will greet you with traditional welcome. Transfer to your hotel in Kathmandu. After settling in, attend a comprehensive tour briefing where our guide will explain the adventure-packed itinerary ahead. Evening free to relax after your journey or explore the vibrant streets of Thamel.

Meals: Not Included (Dinner on your own)

Accommodation: Hotel in Kathmandu

Travel: Airport transfer only

DAY 2 KATHMANDU FULL DAY SIGHTSEEING

UNESCO Heritage Exploration: Full day exploring Kathmandu's cultural treasures. Begin with Kathmandu Durbar Square, a living museum of palaces, courtyards, and temples. Visit the Kumari Ghar to see the living goddess. Proceed to Swayambhunath (Monkey Temple) for panoramic views. After lunch, explore Pashupatinath Temple, Nepal's most sacred Hindu shrine. Continue to Boudhanath Stupa, one of the largest spherical stupas in the world. Evening free for personal exploration.

Meals: Breakfast Included

Accommodation: Hotel in Kathmandu

Sightseeing: 6-7 hours

DAY 3 TRISHULI RIVER RAFTING & BANDIPUR STAY 1,030m

River Adventure: Early morning drive to Trishuli River (approx. 3 hours). After safety briefing and equipment distribution, embark on an exciting white-water rafting adventure through rapids and scenic gorges. Enjoy lunch by the riverside. After rafting, continue drive to Bandipur, a beautifully preserved Newari town. Explore the medieval streets, traditional houses, and enjoy sunset views over the Himalayas. Overnight in a heritage hotel.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner Included

Accommodation: Heritage Hotel in Bandipur

Rafting Duration: 2-3 hours

Drive Duration: 5-6 hours total

DAY 4 DRIVE TO POKHARA & SIGHTSEEING 827m

Scenic Journey: Morning exploration of Bandipur. After breakfast, drive to Pokhara (approx. 3 hours), Nepal's adventure capital. Upon arrival, check into your hotel. Afternoon Pokhara sightseeing including Davis Falls, Gupteshwor Cave, and the International Mountain Museum. Evening visit to Phewa Lake for stunning reflections of the Annapurna range. Enjoy lakeside dinner with mountain views.

Meals: Breakfast, Dinner Included

Accommodation: Hotel in Pokhara

Drive Duration: 3 hours

Sightseeing: 3-4 hours

DAY 5 KUSHMA BUNGEE JUMPING & PHEWA LAKE KAYAKING

Adventure Day: Early morning drive to Kushma (approx. 3 hours) for the world's second tallest canyon bungee jump (228m). Experience the ultimate adrenaline rush! After the jump and lunch, return to Pokhara. Afternoon kayaking on Phewa Lake - paddle to the Peace Pagoda for spectacular views. Evening free to explore Pokhara's lively lakeside area.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch Included

Accommodation: Hotel in Pokhara

Drive Duration: 6 hours round trip

Activities: Bungee Jumping (228m), Lake Kayaking (2 hours)

DAY 6 PARAGLIDING & MOUNTAIN CYCLING 1,592m

Sky & Land Adventure: Morning drive to Sarangkot for tandem paragliding with professional pilots. Soar above Pokhara valley with breathtaking views of the Annapurna range and Phewa Lake. After landing, enjoy lunch with a view. Afternoon mountain cycling adventure from Sarangkot to Pame village through scenic trails and traditional villages. Return to Pokhara for overnight stay.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch Included

Accommodation: Hotel in Pokhara

Activities: Paragliding (20-30 min flight), Mountain Cycling (3-4 hours)

DAY 7 TREK TO AUSTRALIAN BASE CAMP 1,740m

Himalayan Trek Begins: Morning drive to Phedi (approx. 1 hour). Begin trekking to Australian Base Camp through beautiful rhododendron forests and traditional Gurung villages. The moderate trek takes 4-5 hours with stunning mountain views. Arrive at Australian Base Camp in time for sunset over the Annapurna range. Overnight in a basic mountain lodge with spectacular panoramic views.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner Included

Accommodation: Mountain Lodge at Australian Base Camp

Trek Duration: 4-5 hours

Trek Distance: 8 km

DAY 8 TREK TO KANDE & ZIP-FLYER ADVENTURE

Morning Trek & High-Speed Adventure: Early morning sunrise view over the Annapurna range. After breakfast, trek down to Kande village (approx. 3 hours). Drive to Sarangkot for the Zip-Flyer experience - one of the world's steepest, longest, and fastest zip lines! Fly at 140+ km/hr over the Pokhara valley. Return to Pokhara for overnight stay.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch Included

Accommodation: Hotel in Pokhara

Trek Duration: 3 hours downhill

Activities: Zip-Flyer (1.8 km, 56% gradient, 140+ km/hr)

DAY 9 FLY TO KATHMANDU & CULTURAL DINNER

Return to Kathmandu: Morning flight from Pokhara to Kathmandu (25 minutes) with spectacular mountain views. Upon arrival, transfer to hotel. Free day for last-minute shopping or optional sightseeing. Evening farewell Nepali cultural dinner with traditional music and dance performances. Celebrate the completion of your adventure-packed Nepal journey.

Meals: Breakfast, Farewell Dinner Included

Accommodation: Hotel in Kathmandu

Flight Duration: 25 minutes

DAY 10 DEPARTURE FROM NEPAL

Journey Ends: After breakfast, transfer to Tribhuvan International Airport for your onward journey. Our representative will assist with check-in procedures. Depart with unforgettable memories of Nepal's cultural treasures, adventure activities, and Himalayan beauty.

Meals: Breakfast Included

Thank you for choosing Honest Travel Tours. Safe travels and hope to see you again in Nepal!

Kushma Bungee Jumping Day Trip

Experience the World's Second Tallest Canyon Bungee

Adventure Overview

Experience the ultimate adrenaline rush at Kushma Bungee - the world's second tallest canyon bungee jump at 228 meters! This day trip from Pokhara takes you to one of Nepal's most thrilling adventure destinations, offering breathtaking views and an unforgettable free-fall experience over the stunning Modi Khola river canyon.

World Record Features

  • Second Tallest Canyon Bungee: 228 meters (748 feet) drop
  • Longest Suspension Bridge: 520 meters connecting Parbat and Baglung districts
  • Double Safety System: Dual rope system with international standards
  • Professional Team: Internationally certified jump masters
  • Stunning Location: Over the Modi Khola river with canyon views
  • Perfect Day Trip: Complete experience from Pokhara in one day

Important Notice

This activity requires good physical health. Participants must be between 14-65 years old, weigh 40-120 kg, and have no heart conditions, high blood pressure, or back problems.

Trip Facts

  • Duration: Full Day (Approx 8-9 hours)
  • Starting Point: Your Hotel in Pokhara
  • Transportation: Private Vehicle with AC
  • Activity Duration: Actual jump: 3-5 seconds, Total experience: 2-3 hours
  • Difficulty: Extreme (Adventure Activity)

Detailed Itinerary

MORNING Drive to Kushma from Pokhara 3 hours

07:00 AM - Morning Departure: After breakfast at your hotel, our representative will pick you up in a private vehicle. The scenic drive from Pokhara to Kushma takes approximately 3 hours through beautiful countryside, traditional villages, and along river valleys. Enjoy the changing landscapes as you approach the adventure destination.

Duration: 3 hours
Distance: 60 km
Meal: Breakfast at hotel
LATE MORNING Arrival at Kushma & Bungee Preparation 228m

10:00 AM - Arrival and Briefing: Upon reaching Kushma, take a short rest after the drive. You'll receive a comprehensive safety briefing from certified jump masters. They will explain the equipment, jumping procedure, and safety measures. Complete necessary paperwork and get fitted with your harness and safety gear.

Safety Check: Double-check all equipment including harness, ropes, and connection points. Professional staff will guide you through every step and answer any last-minute questions.

MIDDAY The Bungee Jump Experience 3-5 sec

11:00 AM - The Jump: Walk to the jump platform on the world's second tallest bungee bridge. Feel the adrenaline build up as you look down 228 meters into the canyon. On the count of three, take the leap of faith! Experience 3-5 seconds of pure free fall before the bungee cord catches you. Bounce several times while enjoying spectacular views of the canyon and river below.

Recovery: After your jump, you'll be lowered to a recovery boat waiting in the river below. The boat will bring you to the shore where you can celebrate your achievement. Receive your jump certificate as a souvenir of this incredible experience.

Activity Duration: 2-3 hours (including preparation and jump)
AFTERNOON Lunch & Local Exploration 1 hour

01:00 PM - Lunch Break: Enjoy a well-deserved lunch at a local restaurant in Kushma. Sample traditional Nepali cuisine while sharing stories of your jump with fellow adventurers. This is also a great time to relax and let the adrenaline settle.

Optional Bridge Exploration: If you're interested and have energy, visit other suspension bridges in the area. Kushma is famous for its network of bridges connecting hills and villages. Capture photos of the stunning landscape and engineering marvels.

LATE AFTERNOON Return to Pokhara 3 hours

03:00 PM - Departure: Begin your journey back to Pokhara via the same scenic route. The return drive takes approximately 3 hours. You'll have time to reflect on your incredible experience while enjoying the beautiful landscapes.

06:00 PM - Arrival in Pokhara: Arrive back at your hotel in Pokhara, filled with unforgettable memories of your bungee jumping adventure. The rest of the evening is free to relax, celebrate, or explore Pokhara's lakeside area.

Drive Back: 3 hours
Arrival Time: ~6:00 PM

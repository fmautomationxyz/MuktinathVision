Nepal River Rafting Adventure: Multi-River Expedition

9 Nights 10 Days

Package Overview

Embark on the ultimate white-water adventure across Nepal's most thrilling rivers. This comprehensive 10-day rafting expedition takes you through three distinct river systems, combining adrenaline-pumping rapids with cultural exploration and scenic beauty. From the technical challenges of Bhote Koshi to the classic Trishuli and scenic Seti River, experience the best of Nepal's rafting diversity.

Key Highlights

  • Three River Challenge: Conquer Bhote Koshi, Trishuli, and Seti rivers with varying difficulty levels
  • Professional Guidance: Expert rafting guides with international safety certifications
  • Cultural Immersion: Visit UNESCO World Heritage sites in Kathmandu and Bhaktapur
  • Scenic Beauty: Raft through breathtaking gorges, jungle corridors, and mountain landscapes
  • Optional Activities: Kayaking, SUP, bungee jumping, and mountain biking available
  • Comfortable Accommodation: Quality hotels and riverside resorts throughout the journey

Trip Facts

  • Duration: 9 Nights 10 Days
  • Starting Point: Kathmandu, Nepal
  • River Difficulty: Class III-IV (Bhote Koshi), Class III (Trishuli), Class II-III (Seti)
  • Best Season: September to December & March to May
  • Group Size: 2-12 people per rafting group
  • Age Requirement: Minimum 16 years (14 with guardian)
  • Physical Fitness: Moderate fitness level required

Detailed Itinerary

DAY 1 ARRIVAL IN KATHMANDU

Welcome to Nepal: Arrive at Tribhuvan International Airport where our representative will greet you with traditional welcome. Transfer to your hotel in Thamel, the tourist hub of Kathmandu. After check-in, attend a comprehensive briefing about your rafting adventure. Our team will explain safety procedures, equipment usage, and the itinerary in detail. Evening free to explore Thamel's vibrant streets, shops, and restaurants.

Meals: Welcome Dinner Included

Accommodation: 3-4 Star Hotel in Kathmandu

DAY 2 BHAKTAPUR SIGHTSEEING & DRIVE TO BHOTEKOSHI

Cultural Exploration: After breakfast, visit Bhaktapur Durbar Square, a UNESCO World Heritage site renowned for its well-preserved medieval architecture. Explore ancient temples, palaces, and courtyards dating back to the 12th century. After lunch, embark on a scenic 3-hour drive to Bhotekoshi through beautiful Nepali countryside. Arrive at the riverside camp, check into your accommodation, and attend a comprehensive rafting safety briefing. Evening relaxation by the river with bonfire (weather permitting).

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, and Dinner Included

Accommodation: Riverside Resort at Bhotekoshi

Drive Duration: 3 hours

DAY 3 BHOTEKOSHI RIVER RAFTING

Adrenaline Rush: After breakfast, gear up for your first rafting adventure on the Bhotekoshi River, one of Nepal's steepest and most challenging rivers. The river features continuous Class III-IV rapids with names like "Frog in a Blender," "Cave," and "Liquid Bliss." Your professional guides will provide thorough safety instructions and paddling techniques. Enjoy 3-4 hours of intense white-water action through steep gorges. After conquering the rapids, enjoy a riverside lunch. Optional afternoon activities include cliff jumping or relaxing at the resort.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, and Dinner Included

Accommodation: Riverside Resort at Bhotekoshi

Rafting Duration: 3-4 hours (18km)

River Difficulty: Class III-IV

DAY 4 DRIVE TO KURINTAR, OPTIONAL ACTIVITIES

Scenic Transfer: After breakfast, drive approximately 4 hours to Kurintar, situated along the banks of the Trishuli River. The journey offers spectacular views of terraced fields, traditional villages, and Himalayan foothills. Upon arrival at the resort, check in and enjoy lunch. The afternoon offers optional adventure activities: test your courage with bungee jumping from one of Asia's highest bungee bridges (152m drop), try the spectacular canyon swing, or enjoy mountain biking along scenic trails. Evening at leisure to enjoy resort facilities.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, and Dinner Included

Accommodation: Resort in Kurintar

Drive Duration: 4 hours

Optional Activities: Bungee Jumping, Canyon Swing, Mountain Biking (extra cost)

DAY 5 TRISHULI RIVER RAFTING

Classic Nepali Rafting: Today experience Nepal's most popular rafting river - the Trishuli. After breakfast, drive to the starting point and receive your safety briefing. The Trishuli offers a perfect blend of exciting rapids and calm floats through scenic gorges. Navigate through Class III rapids including "Teen Devi," "Monkey," and "Upset." The river journey takes you past traditional villages where you can observe local life along the banks. Enjoy a riverside lunch break on a sandy beach. Continue rafting to the take-out point, then transfer back to your resort. Evening free to relax or share stories with fellow rafters.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, and Dinner Included

Accommodation: Resort in Kurintar

Rafting Duration: 4-5 hours (25km)

River Difficulty: Class III

DAY 6 DRIVE TO POKHARA, OPTIONAL ACTIVITIES

Journey to the Lake City: After breakfast, embark on a scenic 5-6 hour drive to Pokhara, Nepal's adventure capital. The road follows the Marshyangdi River valley with stunning views of Annapurna range. Upon arrival in Pokhara, check into your lakeside hotel. The afternoon offers optional activities: paragliding from Sarangkot for breathtaking Himalayan views, zip-lining through the forest, or a peaceful boat ride on Phewa Lake. Evening explore the vibrant Lakeside area with its restaurants, cafes, and shops.

Meals: Breakfast and Dinner Included

Accommodation: Lakeside Hotel in Pokhara

Drive Duration: 5-6 hours

Optional Activities: Paragliding, Zip-lining, Boating (extra cost)

DAY 7 SETI RIVER RAFTING

Scenic River Journey: Experience the beautiful Seti River, known for its clear water and scenic surroundings. After an early breakfast, drive approximately 1.5 hours to the starting point. The Seti offers Class II-III rapids perfect for a more relaxed day or for practicing your rafting skills. Float through jungle corridors with opportunities to spot wildlife including various bird species and monkeys. Enjoy a leisurely lunch on a riverbank. The afternoon includes optional kayaking or stand-up paddleboarding instruction on calm sections of the river. Return to Pokhara in the late afternoon.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, and Dinner Included

Accommodation: Lakeside Hotel in Pokhara

Rafting Duration: 4-5 hours (32km)

River Difficulty: Class II-III

Optional: Kayaking or SUP instruction included

DAY 8 POKHARA FULL DAY SIGHTSEEING

Exploring Pokhara: Enjoy a full day exploring Pokhara's natural and cultural attractions. Morning visit to Sarangkot for sunrise views over the Annapurna range (weather permitting). Return for breakfast, then visit the International Mountain Museum to learn about Himalayan expeditions. Continue to Devi's Fall, a fascinating waterfall that disappears into an underground cavern. Visit the Gupteshwor Cave with its sacred Shiva shrine. After lunch, take a boat ride across Phewa Lake to visit the Peace Pagoda on the opposite shore, offering panoramic views of Pokhara valley and mountains. Evening free for last-minute shopping or relaxation.

Meals: Breakfast and Lunch Included

Accommodation: Lakeside Hotel in Pokhara

Sightseeing: Full day with guide

DAY 9 FLY TO KATHMANDU, HALF-DAY SIGHTSEEING

Return to Kathmandu: Morning transfer to Pokhara Airport for a scenic 25-minute flight to Kathmandu (alternative: 6-7 hour drive available). Upon arrival, check into your hotel. Afternoon sightseeing in Kathmandu including Swayambhunath (Monkey Temple) for panoramic city views and Pashupatinath Temple, one of Hinduism's most sacred sites. Explore the bustling markets of Thamel for last-minute souvenirs. Evening farewell dinner with traditional Nepali cultural program, celebrating your successful rafting adventure.

Meals: Breakfast and Farewell Dinner Included

Accommodation: 3-4 Star Hotel in Kathmandu

Flight Duration: 25 minutes

DAY 10 FAREWELL, DEPARTURE

Journey Ends: Enjoy your final breakfast in Nepal. Depending on your flight schedule, you may have some free time for last-minute shopping or exploration. Our representative will assist with hotel check-out and transfer you to Tribhuvan International Airport for your departure flight. Carry with you unforgettable memories of thrilling rapids, scenic beauty, and warm Nepali hospitality.

Meals: Breakfast Included

Until the next adventure! Stay safe and keep paddling!

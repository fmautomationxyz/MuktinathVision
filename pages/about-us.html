Our Story

Born from Passion, Built on Trust

In the heart of the majestic Himalayas, where towering peaks touch the sky and ancient trails whisper stories of adventure, Honest Travel Tours and Expedition was born from a simple yet powerful vision: to share the authentic beauty of Nepal with the world, guided by unwavering principles of honesty, transparency, and genuine care.

For over 15 years, we have walked alongside thousands of travelers from across the globe—from the spiritual pilgrims seeking enlightenment at Mount Kailash and Muktinath, to adventure seekers conquering Everest Base Camp, to families discovering the hidden treasures of Rara Lake and the Forbidden Kingdom of Upper Mustang. Each journey has reinforced our core belief: travel is not just about destinations; it's about transformative experiences built on trust.

"Our name isn't just what we're called—it's our promise. Every interaction, every itinerary, every price quoted reflects our commitment to complete transparency and zero hidden charges."

Unlike many agencies that rely on middlemen and inflate costs, we are deeply rooted in local communities. Our guides are not just employees—they are our family, born and raised in the mountains they lead you through. This direct connection means you experience Nepal through the eyes of those who know its soul, while we maintain fair pricing that benefits both you and the communities we serve.

Today, Honest Travel stands as Nepal's most trusted travel partner, certified by the Nepal Tourism Board and recognized by travelers worldwide for our exceptional service, safety standards, and the personal touch that transforms clients into lifelong friends.

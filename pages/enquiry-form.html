Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best time to visit Nepal for trekking?

The best trekking seasons in Nepal are:

  • Spring (March to May): Perfect weather, blooming rhododendrons, clear mountain views
  • Autumn (September to November): Stable weather, clear skies, best visibility
  • Winter (December to February): Cold but clear, fewer crowds, lower prices
  • Monsoon (June to August): Rainy season, lush greenery, fewer trekkers
What documents are required for Kailash Mansarovar Yatra?

For Kailash Mansarovar pilgrimage, you need:

  • Valid passport with at least 6 months validity
  • Chinese visa
  • Tibet Travel Permit (arranged by us)
  • Aliens Travel Permit
  • Military Permit for restricted areas
  • Recent passport-size photographs

We handle all permit arrangements for our clients.

How fit do I need to be for Everest Base Camp trek?

While you don't need to be an athlete, good physical fitness is essential:

  • Regular walking/hiking experience
  • Able to walk 5-7 hours daily with a daypack
  • Cardiovascular fitness (running, cycling, swimming)
  • Strength training for legs and core
  • We recommend starting training 2-3 months before your trek

How do I prepare for high altitude?

High altitude preparation is crucial for Himalayan treks:

  • Spend 2-3 days acclimatizing in Kathmandu
  • Follow the "climb high, sleep low" principle
  • Stay hydrated (3-4 liters of water daily)
  • Walk slowly and maintain steady pace
  • Consider Diamox after consulting your doctor
  • Recognize symptoms of AMS (headache, nausea, dizziness)
  • Inform your guide immediately if symptoms appear

Do I need travel insurance?

Yes, travel insurance is mandatory for all our treks and tours. Your insurance must cover:

  • Emergency medical evacuation (up to 6000m)
  • Medical expenses (including COVID-19)
  • Trip cancellation and interruption
  • Lost or delayed baggage
  • Personal liability

We require proof of insurance before departure.

Nepal Family Tour Package

8 days • Kathmandu • Pokhara • Chitwan • cultural & wildlife bond

Create lifelong memories with your loved ones – a perfect blend of culture, nature, and wildlife.

Trip Overview – Family Fun in Nepal

When do you think you will ever again get a chance to step outside of your comfort zone, learn from each other, and grow closer as a family? Our Nepal Family Tour Package is designed to provide opportunities for families to connect and bond in a unique and unforgettable way. This 8‑day hassle‑free tour covers Kathmandu’s heritage, Pokhara’s serenity, and Chitwan’s wildlife – perfect for all ages.

✨ What makes this tour package different?

Nepal is not a famous travel destination just because. It is popular among solo travellers, couples, adventure enthusiasts, naturalists, and even families as well. Our Nepal Family Tour Package is designed to let you experience the wonders of Nepal, regardless of age or interests.

Detailed Itinerary

DAY 1 KATHMANDU ARRIVAL

Your Arrival Time (?) Our office representative will meet and greet you at Kathmandu Airport and transfer you to your hotel.

06:00 PM: In the evening, we will meet for a short briefing about your tour programme. If you arrive after 4 p.m., the briefing will be the next morning during breakfast. Any due payment can be settled at that time.

Accommodation: Hotel in Kathmandu

Meals: Not included

Note: If you have an early flight and free time, you can add optional sightseeing today.

DAY 2 KATHMANDU SIGHTSEEING

10:00 AM: After breakfast, your private vehicle arrives for a full day of UNESCO heritage tours. Visit Pashupatinath Temple (sacred Hindu pilgrimage), then Boudhanath Stupa – one of the largest in the world. You can have lunch at a cozy café around Boudhanath.

Continue to Swayambhunath (Monkey Temple) and later Kathmandu Durbar Square with its historic palaces and Kumari Ghar.

05:00 PM: Transfer back to hotel. Evenings free to explore Thamel or dine nearby.

Accommodation: Hotel in Kathmandu

Meals: Breakfast included

Family tip: can replace one site with Garden of Dreams or the zoo if travelling with young kids.

DAY 3 DRIVE TO POKHARA

08:00 AM: Breakfast and then scenic drive to Pokhara (201 km, 6‑7 hrs). On the way you can take an optional Manakamana cable car (₹670/person round trip) for breathtaking views, or enjoy Trishuli river rafting (2‑3 hrs, extra cost).

Arrive Pokhara, check in at hotel. Rest or stroll around lakeside.

Accommodation: Hotel in Pokhara

Meals: Breakfast Only

Note: activities not included in package cost.
DAY 4 SARANGKOT SUNRISE + POKHARA SIGHTSEEING

05:00 AM: Drive to Sarangkot viewpoint for sunrise over the Annapurna range. Return to hotel for breakfast.

10:00 AM: Sightseeing tour: Davis Falls (Patale Chhango), Gupteshwor Mahadev Cave, Bindhyabasini Temple, and boat ride on Phewa Lake to Taal Barahi Temple. After sightseeing, free time to explore Lakeside.

Accommodation: Hotel in Pokhara

Meals: Breakfast Only

DAY 5 DRIVE TO CHITWAN

09:00 AM: After breakfast, drive to Chitwan (171 km, 6‑7 hrs). Check into jungle resort. Later enjoy sunset view from Rapti riverbank and experience Tharu cultural program in the evening.

Accommodation: Hotel in Chitwan

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch & Dinner included

DAY 6 JUNGLE SAFARI ACTIVITIES

07:00 AM: Breakfast then half‑day jeep safari inside Chitwan National Park – spot one‑horned rhino, deer, maybe a tiger! Return for lunch.

03:30 PM: Canoe ride on Rapti River (watch for crocodiles), guided jungle walk, and visit Elephant Breeding Centre.

Accommodation: Hotel in Chitwan

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch & Dinner included

DAY 7 DRIVE TO KATHMANDU – LEISURE

08:00 AM: Drive back to Kathmandu (181 km, 6‑7 hrs). Check into hotel. Free evening for shopping and exploring Thamel on your own.

Optional: Everest Mountain Flight (early morning 06:30, 1 hour, extra cost).

Accommodation: Hotel in Kathmandu

Meals: Breakfast included

DAY 8 FAREWELL DEPARTURE

Enjoy your last breakfast at the hotel. Free time until your flight. Our representative will transfer you to Kathmandu airport 3 hours before departure.

Extend your stay? Just let us know.

Accommodation: Hotel in Kathmandu

Meals: Breakfast included

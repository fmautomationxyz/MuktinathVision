Short Nepal Tour Package 6 Days

Experience Kathmandu's cultural gems and Pokhara's natural beauty

Tour Overview

This 6-day short Nepal tour perfectly blends cultural heritage with natural beauty. Explore Kathmandu's ancient temples and UNESCO World Heritage Sites, then experience Pokhara's stunning lakes and Himalayan views. The journey includes a visit to the sacred Manakamana Temple and a breathtaking sunrise view from Sarangkot.

Detailed Itinerary

DAY 1 KATHMANDU ARRIVAL 1,400m

Arrival in Kathmandu: Our office representative will meet and greet you at Kathmandu Airport and transfer you to your hotel.

Note: If you have an early flight to Kathmandu and have some free time this day, you can add some sightseeing as per your interest.

Meals: Not Included

Accommodation: Hotel in Kathmandu

DAY 2 KATHMANDU SIGHTSEEING

Full Day Heritage Tour: At 09:00 AM: Have your breakfast at the hotel and get ready for today's sightseeing. During a full day sightseeing, you will visit UNESCO-listed world heritage sites such as Pashupatinath Temple, Jal Narayan Temple, Swayambhunath Stupa, Patan Durbar Square, and Guhyeshwari Temple. Once you complete your sightseeing, we will drop you back to your hotel.

Optional Activity: Everest Mountain Flight at 06:30 AM from Kathmandu airport, a 50-minute flight that takes you for an aerial view of Mt. Everest and several other mountains. (₹10,500 Per Person, window seat for all)

Meals: Breakfast Included

Accommodation: Hotel in Kathmandu

Sightseeing: Full day

DAY 3 DRIVE TO POKHARA, VISIT MANAKAMANA TEMPLE

Journey to Pokhara: At 08:00 AM: After your breakfast, proceed to Pokhara. On the way you can visit Manakamana temple by cable car. It takes approx. 3 hours to complete the sightseeing of the temple. After sightseeing you can have your lunch at the Manakamana restaurant, and continue drive to Pokhara.

Note: The cable car cost (₹670 Per Person) isn't included in our given price.

Meals: Breakfast Included

Accommodation: Hotel in Pokhara

Drive Duration: 6-7 hours (including temple visit)

DAY 4 SARANGKOT SUNRISE & POKHARA SIGHTSEEING 1,592m

Sunrise and City Tour: At 05:00 AM: Early in the morning, you will drive to Sarangkot viewpoint for a mesmerizing sunrise view over the towering mountain peaks. The rays of sunrise painted over the sky while the mountain ranges stand golden will leave your jaw dropping.

Enjoy the serene view of the mountain ranges, as well as the most breathtaking sunrise over Pokhara City. Take some shots of the gorgeous Himalayas and sunrise, then return to your hotel for breakfast.

At 10:00 AM: After having your breakfast at the Hotel, you will get a certain time to participate in available optional activities in Pokhara. If you are not interested in any activities then you will visit Davis fall, Gupteshwar Mahadev Cave, Bindhyabasini Temple, Barahi Temple, and Phewa Lake. Besides that, you can enjoy a boat ride at Phewa Lake.

Meals: Breakfast Included

Accommodation: Hotel in Pokhara

DAY 5 DRIVE TO KATHMANDU, LEISURE DAY

Return to Kathmandu: At 08:00 AM: After breakfast, our local vehicle will pick you up from the hotel and drive you back to Kathmandu. Upon reaching the hotel, check in and get some rest. You will have a free evening in Kathmandu. You can use your time for shopping and self exploring around Thamel, a major tourist hub where you can find diverse local shops, restaurants, and cafes. You can have your dinner in the area as per your interest.

Meals: Breakfast Included

Accommodation: Hotel in Kathmandu

Drive Duration: 6-7 hours

DAY 6 TRANSFER TO AIRPORT

Final Day: As you enjoy your last breakfast at the hotel, the time to go back is already here. You can have some time of your own or add some additional activities until your flight time. Our company representative will escort you to the Kathmandu airport 3 hours before your flight.

Meals: Breakfast Included

Have a safe journey back! It will be our pleasure to be part of your journey if you want to extend your stay in Nepal.

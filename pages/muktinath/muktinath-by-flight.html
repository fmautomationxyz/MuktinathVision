5 Nights 6 Days - Fast & Scenic Flight Journey to the Sacred Temple

Fast Flight Journey to Muktinath

Experience a swift and scenic 6-day pilgrimage to the sacred Muktinath Temple via flight, maximizing your time at spiritual sites while enjoying breathtaking aerial views of the Himalayas. This efficiently designed package combines domestic flights with comprehensive sightseeing, offering the fastest route to the "Place of Liberation" while ensuring comfortable travel and meaningful spiritual experiences.

Journey Highlights

  • Multiple Scenic Flights: Kathmandu-Pokhara-Jomsom flights with Himalayan views
  • Time-Efficient Travel: Maximize pilgrimage time with flight transfers
  • Sacred Temple Visit: Complete puja and holy bath at Muktinath's 108 waterspouts
  • Comprehensive Sightseeing: Explore Kathmandu and Pokhara's major attractions
  • Optional Activities: Everest Mountain Flight and Dhumba Lake visit
  • Comfortable Journey: All flights included with private ground transfers

Trip Facts

  • Duration: 5 Nights 6 Days
  • Starting Point: Kathmandu, Nepal
  • Transportation: Domestic Flights + Private Vehicle
  • Maximum Altitude: 3,762m (Muktinath Temple)
  • Difficulty: Easy to Moderate
  • Best Season: Year-round (Spring & Autumn ideal)
  • Flight Duration: 30 min (KTM-PKR) + 20 min (PKR-JOM)
  • Hotel Categories: 3, 4, or 5 Star options available

Why Choose Flight Journey to Muktinath?

The flight journey offers the fastest and most comfortable route to Muktinath, ideal for travelers with limited time or those preferring to avoid long road journeys. You'll enjoy spectacular aerial views of the Himalayas including Annapurna and Dhaulagiri ranges, while saving valuable time for spiritual activities and sightseeing. This option is perfect for seniors, families with children, or anyone seeking a more comfortable pilgrimage experience.

Detailed Itinerary

DAY 1 ARRIVAL IN KATHMANDU 1,400m

Welcome to Nepal: Our office representative will meet and greet you at Kathmandu Airport and transfer you to your hotel. Complete check-in formalities and rest after your journey.

06:00 PM: In the evening, we will meet for a short briefing about your tour programme. If you are arriving late (after 4 p.m.), then we will have a briefing the next morning during breakfast. If any payment is due, it will be collected during the briefing time.

Optional Activity: If you have an early flight to Kathmandu and have some free time this day, you can add some sightseeing as per your interest.

Meals: Not Included

Accommodation: Hotel Moonlight (3 Star)

DAY 2 KATHMANDU SIGHTSEEING, FLY TO POKHARA

07:30 AM: After breakfast at your hotel, you will participate in half-day sightseeing at Pashupatinath Temple and Jal Narayan Temple before flying to Pokhara.

Sightseeing: Your transportation will arrive at the hotel to pick you up for sightseeing:

  • Pashupatinath Temple: Sacred Hindu pilgrimage site dedicated to Lord Shiva. Explore around 492 temples, 15 Shivalayas, and 12 Jyotirlingas.
  • Jal Narayan Temple (Budhanilkantha): Famous for its large reclining statue of Lord Vishnu resting in a water pool.

01:30 PM: After completing your sightseeing, our transportation service will drop you off at Kathmandu airport for the flight to Pokhara, which takes approximately 30 minutes.

Arrival in Pokhara: Once you arrive at Pokhara Airport, our local representative will pick you up and drop you off at your hotel.

Meals: Breakfast Included

Accommodation: White Pearl Hotel

Flight Duration: 30 minutes

Sightseeing Time: 4-5 hours

DAY 3 FLY TO JOMSOM, DRIVE TO MUKTINATH 3,762m

06:30 AM: Today you will take an early morning flight to Jomsom from Pokhara, which is about 15-20 minutes of flying time. Enjoy breathtaking views of Annapurna and Dhaulagiri ranges during the flight.

Arrival in Jomsom: Check-in at the hotel in Jomsom, have your breakfast there, and drive to Muktinath by private jeep.

Journey to Muktinath: The distance between Jomsom and Muktinath is around 23 km, which takes around 1-2 hours to drive. Explore the sacred site, witness religious practices, and take a holy bath in the 108 waterspouts followed by a purifying dip in two ponds around the temple premises.

Important Notes:

  • 30-minute walk uphill from jeep parking to temple
  • Pony ride available at INR 600 for two-way ride (optional, payable directly)
  • Various restaurants available at the base of the temple for lunch

02:00 PM: After Darshan and Pooja, drive back to Jomsom for overnight stay.

Optional Activities in Jomsom: If time permits, you may visit:

  • Mustang Eco-Museum: Houses ancient objects and manuscripts relating to Tibetan culture, herbs, and medicine
  • Dhumba Lake: 3.6 km (2-hour walk) from Jomsom, famous for natural and religious significance

Meals: Breakfast and Dinner Included

Accommodation: Om's Home (Jomsom)

Flight Duration: 15-20 minutes

Drive Duration: 1-2 hours each way

DAY 4 FLY TO POKHARA, POKHARA SIGHTSEEING

06:30 AM: Today, you will fly back to Pokhara. Have your breakfast after the flight in Pokhara.

Note: Early morning check-in at the hotel is subject to room availability since standard check-in time is 2:00 PM.

11:00 AM: Check in, freshen up, and get ready for your sightseeing tour of Pokhara. Today's schedule includes:

  • Davis Falls: 500-foot long waterfall that runs 100 feet below ground level
  • Gupteshwor Mahadev Cave: Nearly 3 km long cave with Shiva statue and waterfall view
  • Bindhyabasini Temple: Sacred temple dedicated to Goddess Bhagwati on hilltop
  • Taal Barahi Temple: Pagoda-style temple on island in Phewa Lake (boat ride included)

After sightseeing, you'll be escorted back to your hotel. Evening free to explore Lakeside area.

Weather Note: Flights from Pokhara to Jomsom depend on weather. We will arrange private 4WD Jeep if flight is cancelled. Group size will affect Jeep price.

Meals: Breakfast Included

Accommodation: White Pearl Hotel

DAY 5 FLY TO KATHMANDU, KATHMANDU SIGHTSEEING

10:00 AM: After breakfast, proceed for your flight to Kathmandu. Our transportation will pick you up from Kathmandu airport.

Sightseeing: You'll go for sightseeing directly from the airport:

  • Patan Durbar Square: UNESCO site with Newari architecture, temples, and palaces. Features Krishna Mandir, Bhimsen Temple, and Patan Museum.
  • Kathmandu Durbar Square: Ancient royal palace site showcasing cultural heritage. Includes Hanuman Dhoka, Kumari Ghar, Kasthamandap, and Taleju Temple.

Once you finish sightseeing, you will be dropped off at your hotel. Check-in and you can rest or explore local markets like Thamel for shopping.

Meals: Breakfast Included

Accommodation: Hotel Moonlight (3 Star)

Flight Duration: 30 minutes

DAY 6 DEPARTURE FROM KATHMANDU

Journey Completion: Enjoy breakfast at the hotel. You'll have free time until your departure flight.

Airport Transfer: Our transportation service will escort you to Kathmandu airport 3 hours before your flight. We'll assist with check-in procedures.

Optional Activity: Everest Mountain Flight at 06:30 AM - 1-hour aerial tour of Himalayas including Mt. Everest, Lhotse, Kanchenjunga, and other peaks (additional cost).

Extension Options: We can arrange additional tours to Chitwan National Park, Lumbini (Birthplace of Buddha), or other destinations if you wish to extend your stay.

Meals: Breakfast Included

May Lord Vishnu's blessings of Mukti (liberation) be with you always!

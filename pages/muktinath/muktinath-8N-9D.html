Detailed Itinerary

DAY 1 ARRIVAL IN KATHMANDU 1,400m

Welcome to Nepal: Upon arrival at Tribhuvan International Airport, our representative will warmly welcome you with traditional greetings. Transfer to your hotel in a comfortable private vehicle. Complete check-in formalities and rest after your journey.

Evening: Briefing about the upcoming spiritual journey. Free time for rest or optional exploration of nearby areas.

Meals: Dinner Included

Accommodation: 3 Star Hotel in Kathmandu

DAY 2 KATHMANDU TO JANAKPUR 78m

Journey to Janakpur: After breakfast, begin your scenic drive to Janakpur (approximately 8-9 hours). Janakpur is the birthplace of Goddess Sita and an important pilgrimage site mentioned in the Ramayana.

Arrival: Upon arrival, check into your hotel. Evening free for rest after the long journey.

Meals: Breakfast & Dinner Included

Accommodation: 3 Star Hotel in Janakpur

Drive Duration: 8-9 hours

Distance: 375 km

DAY 3 JANAKPUR DARSHAN & DRIVE TO POKHARA

Morning Temple Visit: After breakfast, visit the sacred Janaki Temple (also known as Naulakha Temple). This magnificent temple is dedicated to Goddess Sita and is an architectural marvel built in 1910 AD.

Other Sites: Visit Ram Temple, Vivah Mandap (marriage pavilion), and other important religious sites in Janakpur.

Afternoon: After completing darshan, begin your drive to Pokhara (approximately 6-7 hours). Enjoy scenic views of hills and rivers along the way.

Meals: Breakfast & Dinner Included

Accommodation: 3 Star Hotel in Pokhara

DAY 4 POKHARA SIGHTSEEING

Exploration Day: After breakfast, begin full day sightseeing of Pokhara's major attractions. Visit Tal Barahi Temple situated on an island in Phewa Lake (boat ride included).

Other Sites: Witness the spectacular Davis Falls where water disappears underground, explore the mystical Gupteshwar Mahadev Cave with its natural Shiva Linga, and seek blessings at Bindhyabasini Temple.

Evening: Free to relax by the lake, explore Pokhara's markets, or enjoy cultural shows.

Meals: Breakfast & Dinner Included

Accommodation: 3 Star Hotel in Pokhara

DAY 5 DRIVE TO MUKTINATH VIA TATOPANI

Journey to the Mountains: Early morning departure for Muktinath (approximately 8-9 hours). Drive through beautiful mountain scenery along the Kali Gandaki River valley.

Tatopani Hot Spring: Stop at Tatopani (meaning "hot water") for a relaxing natural hot spring bath. The sulfuric waters are believed to have healing properties.

Arrival: Continue drive to Muktinath area. Check into hotel in Jomsom or nearby area for overnight stay.

Note: This is a long drive day through mountain roads. Carry warm clothing as temperature drops significantly.

Meals: Breakfast & Dinner Included

Accommodation: Hotel in Jomsom area

DAY 6 MUKTINATH DARSHAN & RETURN TO POKHARA

Sacred Day: Early morning drive to Muktinath Temple. Complete Muktinath Temple Darshan, perform puja rituals, and take the holy bath under 108 waterspouts (Mukti Dhara).

Important: After getting off the vehicle, you have to walk up for 30 minutes to reach the temple from parking area. You can hire a pony on your own (INR 800 for two way ride).

Afternoon: After completing spiritual activities, begin return journey to Pokhara (approximately 8-9 hours).

Meals: Breakfast & Dinner Included

Accommodation: 3 Star Hotel in Pokhara

DAY 7 DRIVE TO KATHMANDU

Return Journey: After breakfast, drive back to Kathmandu (approximately 6-7 hours). Enjoy scenic views of hills, rivers, and villages along the Prithvi Highway.

Optional Stop: You can opt to visit Manakamana Temple via cable car enroute (additional cost).

Arrival: Upon reaching Kathmandu, check into hotel. Evening free for rest or shopping.

Meals: Breakfast & Dinner Included

Accommodation: 3 Star Hotel in Kathmandu

DAY 8 KATHMANDU SIGHTSEEING

Cultural Exploration: After breakfast, begin full day sightseeing of Kathmandu's UNESCO World Heritage Sites. Visit the sacred Pashupatinath Temple, one of the most important Shiva temples in the world.

Other Sites: Visit Boudhanath Stupa (largest stupa in Nepal), Swayambhunath Stupa (Monkey Temple), and Patan Durbar Square with its ancient palaces and temples.

Evening: Farewell dinner with cultural program. Free time for last-minute shopping in Thamel.

Meals: Breakfast & Dinner Included

Accommodation: 3 Star Hotel in Kathmandu

DAY 9 DEPARTURE FROM KATHMANDU

Journey Completion: Enjoy a leisurely breakfast at your hotel. Free time until your departure flight for last-minute packing or optional activities.

Transfer: Our transportation service will pick you up from the hotel 3 hours before your flight time and transfer you to Tribhuvan International Airport. We'll assist with check-in procedures for your onward journey.

Meals: Breakfast Included

May Lord Vishnu's blessings of Mukti (liberation) be with you always! Safe journey back home.

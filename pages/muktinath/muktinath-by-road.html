Muktinath Tour Package by Road

6 Nights 7 Days - Complete Road Journey to the Sacred Temple of Liberation

Complete Road Journey to Muktinath

Embark on a comprehensive 7-day spiritual pilgrimage to the sacred Muktinath Temple via road, experiencing the breathtaking landscapes of Nepal from Kathmandu to the Himalayas. This thoughtfully designed package offers a complete road journey with scenic drives, cultural immersion, and spiritual experiences, taking you through diverse terrains and local communities to reach the "Place of Liberation."

Journey Highlights

  • Complete Road Experience: Scenic drives through Nepal's diverse landscapes
  • Pashupatinath Evening Aarti: Witness the sacred fire ceremony at Nepal's holiest Shiva temple
  • Pokhara Natural Wonders: Explore Davis Falls, Gupteshwor Cave, and Phewa Lake
  • Enroute Attractions: Visit Marpha Village, Rupse Waterfall, and Kali Gandaki River
  • Spiritual Immersion: Complete puja and holy bath at Muktinath's 108 waterspouts
  • Cable Car Option: Optional Manakamana Temple visit on return journey

Trip Facts

  • Duration: 6 Nights 7 Days
  • Starting Point: Kathmandu, Nepal
  • Transportation: Private AC Vehicle + Domestic Flight (Kathmandu-Pokhara)
  • Maximum Altitude: 3,762m (Muktinath Temple)
  • Difficulty: Moderate
  • Best Season: Spring (Mar-May) & Autumn (Sep-Nov)
  • Temple Significance: One of the 108 Divya Desams of Lord Vishnu
  • Total Driving Distance: Approximately 550 km

Why Choose Road Journey to Muktinath?

The road journey to Muktinath offers a unique opportunity to experience Nepal's diverse landscapes, from fertile valleys to arid mountains. You'll pass through traditional villages, witness local lifestyles, and visit multiple attractions along the route. This comprehensive journey allows gradual acclimatization and deeper cultural immersion compared to flight options, making your pilgrimage more meaningful and memorable.

Detailed Itinerary

DAY 1 ARRIVAL IN KATHMANDU & PASHUPATINATH VISIT 1,400m

Welcome to Nepal: Our office representative will meet and greet you at Kathmandu Airport and transfer you to your hotel. Complete check-in formalities and rest after your journey.

04:30 PM: In the evening, you will visit Pashupatinath Temple for the evening aarti. Witness the priest performing the aarti, taking oil lamps and moving them in circular motions, and devotees creating blissful surroundings. If you are arriving late, then you will visit Pashupatinath on the sixth day to see the aarti.

About Pashupatinath Temple: The largest and holiest shrine for Hindus, dedicated to Lord Shiva. This temple, situated on the bank of the Bagmati River, was built in the 5th century. It is represented by a four faced shiva-lingam housed in the main temple. There are around 492 temples, 15 Shivalayas, and 12 Jyotirlingas to explore.

Note: Only Hindus are allowed to enter the temple, but visitors can clearly see the temple activities from the eastern bank of the Bagmati River.

Meals: Not Included

Accommodation: Arts Kathmandu (3 Star Hotel)

DAY 2 FLY TO POKHARA & SIGHTSEEING 822m

09:00 AM: After breakfast, our transportation service will pick you up from the hotel and drop you off at Kathmandu airport for the flight to Pokhara, which takes approximately 30 minutes. Once you arrive at Pokhara Airport, our local representative will pick you up and drop you off at your hotel.

11:00 AM: Check in, refresh yourself, and get ready for your sightseeing tour of Pokhara. Today's schedule will take you to the most natural and culturally significant landmarks in Pokhara:

  • Pumdikot Shiva Statue: 51-foot tall statue with 108 Shiva lingas and panoramic Himalayan views
  • Bindhyabasini Temple: Sacred temple dedicated to Goddess Bhagwati on a hilltop
  • Davis Falls: Powerful waterfall that runs 100 feet below ground level
  • Gupteshwor Mahadev Cave: Nearly 3 km long cave with Shiva statue and waterfall
  • Taal Barahi Temple: Pagoda-style temple on an island in Phewa Lake (boat ride included)

After sightseeing, you'll be escorted back to your hotel. Evening free to explore Lakeside area.

Meals: Breakfast Included

Accommodation: White Pearl Hotel (3 Star)

Flight Duration: 30 minutes

DAY 3 DRIVE TO MUKTINATH 3,762m

07:30 AM: After having your breakfast, our vehicle will arrive at the hotel for your drive towards today's destination, Muktinath. The distance between Pokhara and Muktinath is around 175 km, which takes approximately 6-7 hours by drive, depending on road conditions.

Journey Highlights: The stunning landscapes, rivers flowing alongside, waterfalls, and diverse communities passing by will astound you on your journey.

02:00 PM: Check in at the hotel, refresh yourself, and you will go for your visit to the temple. As you reach the temple, you can explore the sacred site, witness the religious practices, and also take a holy bath in the 108 waterspouts behind the temple, followed by a purifying dip in the two ponds in front of the temple.

Important Notes:

  • 30-minute walk uphill from jeep parking to temple
  • Pony ride available at INR 600 round trip (optional, payable directly)
  • Carry warm clothing as temperatures drop significantly
  • Various restaurants and cafes available at the base of the temple

About Muktinath Temple: A Vishnu temple sacred for both Hindus and Buddhists, located at the foot of Thorong La Pass. It is one of the 108 Divya Desams for Hindus and one of the 24 tantric places for Buddhists.

Meals: Breakfast Included

Accommodation: Face Resort (Muktinath)

Drive Duration: 6-7 hours

Distance: 175 km

DAY 4 DRIVE TO POKHARA WITH ENROUTE SIGHTSEEING 822m

05:30 AM: Wake up early to witness the glorious sunrise accompanied by panoramic Himalayan views. Enjoy breakfast with surrounding views.

08:00 AM: Today, you will depart for Pokhara, stopping at key attractions on the route back:

  • Marpha Village: 26 km from Muktinath, famous for apple farms and traditional architecture
  • Rupse Waterfall: Majestic 300-meter waterfall along Beni-Jomsom Highway
  • Kali Gandaki River: One of the deepest gorges in the world, sacred to Hindus and Buddhists
  • Baglung Kalika Temple OR Kusma Suspension Bridge: Visit one of these two attractions (time permitting)

About the Attractions:

  • Kusma Suspension Bridge: World's highest suspension bridge (344m long, 117m high)
  • Baglung Kalika Temple: Significant Hindu temple with multiple smaller temples

06:00 PM: Arrive in Pokhara, check-in at hotel. Evening free to relax or explore Lakeside.

Meals: Breakfast Included

Accommodation: White Pearl Hotel (3 Star)

Drive Duration: 6-7 hours with stops

DAY 5 DRIVE TO KATHMANDU VIA MANAKAMANA 1,400m

08:00 AM: Enjoy a fulfilling breakfast at the hotel and set out to be back in Kathmandu. The distance between Pokhara and Kathmandu is about 201 km, which takes about 6 to 7 hours by drive.

Optional Activity: On the way back, you can visit Manakamana Temple by cable car. The cable car ride offers beautiful views of the surrounding landscape. You can also have lunch near the temple area.

Note: The Manakamana cable car ticket price is INR 670 per person for a two-way ride. You can pay for tickets at the base station, Kurintar.

About Manakamana Temple: Popular temple of Goddess Durga Bhagawati in Gorkha district. The name means "heart's wish" - believed that Goddess fulfills wishes of those who worship with pure soul.

Evening: Once you reach Kathmandu, check in at the hotel and rest or explore the city.

Meals: Breakfast Included

Accommodation: Arts Kathmandu (3 Star Hotel)

Drive Duration: 6-7 hours

Distance: 201 km

DAY 6 KATHMANDU SIGHTSEEING 1,400m

10:00 AM: Have breakfast at the hotel and get ready for your sightseeing tour.

Sightseeing Itinerary:

  • Pashupatinath Temple: If you arrived late on Day 1, visit now. Otherwise visit Guhyeshwari Temple nearby
  • Guhyeshwari Temple: Dedicated to Adhi Shakti, important for Tantric worshippers
  • Jal Narayan Temple (Budhanilkantha): Large reclining statue of Lord Vishnu in water pool
  • Patan Durbar Square: UNESCO site with Newari architecture, temples, and palaces
  • Swayambhunath Stupa (Monkey Temple): Oldest Buddhist shrine with panoramic city views

About the Sites:

  • Guhyeshwari: Beautiful pagoda-style architecture with unique flat idol
  • Jal Narayan: Open-air shrine with serene atmosphere, 8 km north of Kathmandu
  • Patan Durbar Square: Features Krishna Mandir, Bhimsen Temple, and Patan Museum
  • Swayambhunath: Dates back 2500 years, shows Hindu-Buddhist harmony

05:00 PM: Finish sightseeing and return to hotel. Evening free for shopping in Thamel or dining at Durbar Marg.

Meals: Breakfast Included

Accommodation: Arts Kathmandu (3 Star Hotel)

DAY 7 DEPARTURE FROM KATHMANDU

Journey Completion: Enjoy breakfast at the hotel. You'll have free time until your departure flight.

Airport Transfer: Our transportation service will pick you up from the hotel 3 hours before your flight and transfer you to Tribhuvan International Airport. We'll assist with check-in procedures.

Optional Activity: Everest Mountain Flight at 06:30 AM - 1-hour aerial tour of Himalayas including Mt. Everest, Lhotse, Kanchenjunga, and other peaks (additional cost).

Extension Options: We can arrange additional tours to Chitwan National Park, Lumbini (Birthplace of Buddha), or other destinations if you wish to extend your stay.

Meals: Breakfast Included

May Lord Vishnu's blessings of Mukti (liberation) be with you always!

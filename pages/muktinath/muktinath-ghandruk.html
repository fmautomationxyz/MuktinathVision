Detailed Itinerary

DAY 1 ARRIVAL IN KATHMANDU 1,400m

Welcome to Nepal: Our representative will meet and greet you at Kathmandu airport and transfer you to your hotel. Complete check-in formalities and rest after your journey.

Note: If you have an early flight to Kathmandu and have some free time this day, you can add some sightseeing as per your interest.

Meals: None Included

Accommodation: 3 Star Hotel in Kathmandu

DAY 2 DRIVE TO POKHARA, VISIT MANAKAMANA TEMPLE 822m

Scenic Drive with Optional Temple Visit: At 08:00 AM: Enjoy breakfast and drive to Pokhara (201 km, 6-7 hours). Optional: Visit Manakamana Temple by cable car for beautiful views of the landscape. You can also participate in river rafting on the Trishuli River.

Note: Manakamana cable car tickets are INR 670/person for a round trip, purchasable at the Kurintar base station.

At 04:00 PM: Reach Pokhara, check into hotel, and rest or explore the city.

Meals: Breakfast & Dinner Included

Accommodation: 3 Star Hotel in Pokhara

Drive Duration: 6-7 hours

DAY 3 DRIVE TO MUKTINATH, TEMPLE DARSHAN 3,710m

Sacred Pilgrimage Day: At 07:30 AM: Drive to Muktinath temple (180 km, 6-7 hours). Complete Muktinath Temple Darshan, Puja and Holy bath under 108 waterspouts.

Important Note: After getting off the jeep, you have to walk up for 30 minutes to reach the temple from Jeep parking. You can hire a pony on your own, which costs INR 800 for a two way ride.

After temple visit, drive down to Jomsom for overnight stay.

Meals: Breakfast & Dinner Included

Accommodation: Basic Hotel in Jomsom

Drive Duration: 6-7 hours

DAY 4 DRIVE TO GHANDRUK, VILLAGE EXPLORATION 2,012m

Traditional Village Experience: At 08:00 AM: After breakfast, drive to Ghandruk village (120 km, 4-5 hours). Walk about 30 minutes from vehicle point to reach your local lodge.

At 03:00 PM: Visit the village by walking and enjoy the sunset over the mountains. Experience authentic Gurung culture and hospitality.

Meals: Breakfast & Dinner Included

Accommodation: Local Lodge in Ghandruk

Drive Duration: 4-5 hours

Walking: 30 minutes to lodge

DAY 5 SUNRISE, DRIVE TO POKHARA, SIGHTSEEING

Mountain Sunrise & City Exploration: At 05:30 AM: Wake up early and view the sunrise from your hotel rooftop over Annapurna range.

At 09:00 AM: After breakfast, drive to Pokhara (55 km, 2 hours).

At 11:00 AM: Check into hotel, refresh, and get ready for sightseeing.

At 12:00 PM: Visit Davis Fall, Gupteshwar Mahadev Cave, Bindhyabasini Temple, Barahi Temple, and Phewa Lake. Enjoy a boat ride at Phewa Lake.

Meals: Breakfast & Dinner Included

Accommodation: 3 Star Hotel in Pokhara

DAY 6 DRIVE TO KATHMANDU VIA CHANDRAGIRI HILL

Scenic Return with Cable Car Experience: At 08:00 AM: After breakfast, drive back to Kathmandu. Stop at Chandragiri hill for cable car ride (9 minutes to top).

Experience the religious significance at Bhaleshwor temple with serene atmosphere and panoramic views.

Note: The two way cable car ride to Chandragiri Hills is NPR 1280/INR 840. Purchase at base station.

At 06:00 PM: Arrive in Kathmandu, check into hotel. Evening free for rest or personal exploration.

Meals: Breakfast & Dinner Included

Accommodation: 3 Star Hotel in Kathmandu

DAY 7 KATHMANDU SIGHTSEEING

UNESCO Heritage Exploration: At 09:00 AM: After breakfast, full day sightseeing of UNESCO World Heritage sites: Patan Durbar Square, Swayambhunath Stupa (Monkey Temple), Pashupatinath Temple, Shakti Peeth, and Budhanilkantha.

Optional Activity: Everest Mountain Flight at 06:30 AM - 50-minute aerial view of Mt. Everest and Himalayas (INR 10,500 Per Person, window seat for all).

At 05:00 PM: Transfer back to hotel. Free evening for shopping or relaxation.

Meals: Breakfast & Dinner Included

Accommodation: 3 Star Hotel in Kathmandu

DAY 8 TRANSFER TO AIRPORT, FAREWELL

Departure Day: At 08:00 AM: Enjoy your last breakfast at the hotel. Free time for personal activities or last-minute shopping.

Our company representative will escort you to Kathmandu airport 3 hours before your flight. Have a safe journey back!

Meals: Breakfast Included

It will be our pleasure to be part of your journey if you want to extend your stay in Nepal.

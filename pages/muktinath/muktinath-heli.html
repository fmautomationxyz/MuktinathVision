Spiritual Sites Visit:

Pashupatinath Temple:

Nepal's largest and holiest Hindu shrine, situated along the Bagmati River. Built in the 5th century, this UNESCO site features 492 temples, 15 Shivalayas, and 12 Jyotirlingas. While only Hindus may enter the main temple, all visitors can observe rituals from the eastern bank. SAARC nationals enjoy free entry.

Jal Narayan Temple (Budhanilkantha):

Located 9 km north of Kathmandu, this temple features a magnificent stone statue of sleeping Lord Vishnu appearing to float on water. Dedicated to Lord Vishnu, it's a serene spiritual site away from the city bustle.

02:00 PM:

Fly from Kathmandu to Pokhara. Upon arrival, transfer to the exclusive Fishtail Lodge. Evening free to enjoy Pokhara's lakeside tranquility.

Embark on your exclusive chartered helicopter flight from Pokhara to Muktinath. Enjoy breathtaking aerial views of the Annapurna and Dhaulagiri mountain ranges.

Muktinath Temple Experience:

Spend approximately 1 hour at the sacred temple performing darshan and taking the holy bath under 108 waterspouts. According to legend, Lord Vishnu attained salvation here from Brinda (wife of Jalandhar). The pagoda-style temple offers spiritual cleansing, with two sacred ponds for ritual bathing. The dramatic mountain landscape adds to the divine atmosphere.

Return Flight:

Return to Pokhara by the same helicopter, completing your pilgrimage in comfort.

01:00 PM:

Patan Durbar Square:

Located 5 km southeast of Kathmandu, this UNESCO site features magnificent temples including Krishna Temple, Taleju Temple, and Bhimsen Temple. Explore Patan Museum, Mahaboudha, Kumbeshwor, and traditional courtyards.

Banglamukhi Temple:

One of Kathmandu's most famous Goddess temples, located in Patan. The temple complex contains several smaller shrines. Thursday is particularly auspicious for visits.

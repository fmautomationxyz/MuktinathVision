Complete Spiritual Journey from India to Nepal

Embark on an unforgettable 8-day spiritual pilgrimage starting from Gorakhpur, India, visiting the sacred birthplace of Buddha in Lumbini, and culminating at the divine Muktinath Temple in the Himalayas. This comprehensive package combines the spiritual significance of Lumbini with the liberation blessing of Muktinath, offering a complete Hindu-Buddhist pilgrimage experience across borders.

Journey Highlights

  • Two Sacred Pilgrimages: Visit Buddha's birthplace in Lumbini + Muktinath Temple of Liberation
  • Cross-Border Experience: Journey from India (Gorakhpur) to Nepal (Lumbini, Pokhara, Muktinath)
  • Scenic Mountain Flight: Fly over Himalayas from Pokhara to Jomsom
  • Comprehensive Sightseeing: Explore Lumbini, Pokhara, and Kathmandu's UNESCO sites
  • Optional Cable Car Ride: Visit Manakamana Temple via scenic cable car
  • Complete Spiritual Package: Hindu and Buddhist pilgrimage combined

Trip Facts

  • Duration: 7 Nights 8 Days
  • Starting Point: Gorakhpur, India
  • Ending Point: Gorakhpur, India
  • Transportation: Flight + Private Vehicle
  • Maximum Altitude: 3,762m (Muktinath Temple)
  • Difficulty: Moderate
  • Best Season: October to June
  • Border Crossing: India-Nepal land border
  • Total Driving Distance: Approximately 1,000 km

Why Choose This Package?

This unique package offers a rare opportunity to combine two of the most sacred pilgrimages in South Asia. Starting from the Indian border, you'll visit Lumbini - the birthplace of Lord Buddha, then proceed to Muktinath - where Lord Vishnu grants salvation. Perfect for devotees seeking comprehensive spiritual experiences, this journey covers Hindu and Buddhist holy sites while experiencing the cultural diversity of Nepal from plains to Himalayas.

Detailed Itinerary

DAY 1 GORAKHPUR ARRIVAL, TRANSFER TO LUMBINI 100m

Welcome to Gorakhpur: Our office representative or driver will pick you up from the Gorakhpur airport/railway stations and transfer to Lumbini, Nepal.

Border Crossing: Complete border formalities at India-Nepal border and continue to Lumbini.

Arrival in Lumbini: Upon reaching Lumbini, check-in at the hotel and rest for your tomorrow's drive to Pokhara.

Note: Please carry valid passport/ID proof for border crossing. Indian nationals can use Voter ID card.

Meals: Not Included

Accommodation: Bodhi Red Sun Hotel (Lumbini)

Drive Duration: 3-4 hours

Distance: Approximately 150 km

DAY 2 DRIVE TO POKHARA 822m

08:00 AM: After your breakfast, start driving towards Pokhara (Approx. 7-8 hrs driving).

Scenic Journey: Enjoy the scenic drive through Nepal's countryside, passing through towns and villages with beautiful landscapes.

Arrival in Pokhara: After reaching Pokhara, check in at the hotel and take a rest for the next day's early morning flight to Jomsom.

Evening: Free time to explore Pokhara's Lakeside area or relax at the hotel.

Meals: As Mentioned (Breakfast Included)

Accommodation: White Pearl Hotel (Pokhara)

Drive Duration: 7-8 hours

Distance: Approximately 250 km

DAY 3 FLY TO JOMSOM, DRIVE TO MUKTINATH 3,762m

06:00 AM: Today, early in the morning you will fly to Jomsom. Enjoy breathtaking views of Annapurna and Dhaulagiri ranges during the 15-20 minute flight.

Arrival in Jomsom: Have your breakfast at the hotel in Jomsom.

Journey to Muktinath: Drive to Muktinath temple by private jeep/car. Take a full day for your Puja at the temple.

Spiritual Activities: Perform Darshan, Puja and take Holy bath in the 108 waterspouts and two ponds at the temple.

04:00 PM: Complete your spiritual activities and drive back to Jomsom for the night stay.

About Muktinath Temple: Believed to be a place where Lord Vishnu got salvation from Brinda (wife of Jalandhar). The main shrine is a pagoda shaped temple dedicated to Lord Vishnu. It is believed that one washes away all the pain and sins if they take shower in holy water coming from 108 sprouts just behind the temple.

Meals: Breakfast Included

Accommodation: Om's Home (Jomsom)

Flight Duration: 15-20 minutes

Drive Duration: 1-2 hours each way

DAY 4 FLY BACK TO POKHARA, POKHARA SIGHTSEEING 822m

07:30 AM: Early in the morning, fly back to Pokhara. We will transfer you to your previously stayed hotel.

After Breakfast: You will visit for a half day of sightseeing which includes:

  • Tal Barahi: A temple situated on a small island in the Phewa Lake (boat ride included)
  • Davi's Fall: Famous waterfall in Pokhara Valley
  • Gupteshwor Mahadev Cave: Mystical cave with Shiva statue
  • Bindhyabasini Temple: Sacred temple dedicated to Goddess Bhagwati

Important Note: The weather condition of Jomsom decides the flight status. If your flight is canceled today, we will arrange the land transport for you which is the equivalent of the flight ticket cost up to Pokhara. The road condition is mixed - Ghansa to Beni (40 KM) is completely off road and Beni to Kushma (25 KM) is under construction. While returning by driving you have to pay a TIMS permit fee (INR 400 Per Person) on the way at the checkpoint.

Meals: Breakfast Included

Accommodation: White Pearl Hotel (Pokhara)

DAY 5 DRIVE TO KATHMANDU VIA MANAKAMANA 1,400m

08:00 AM: After your breakfast, proceed to Kathmandu.

Optional Stop: On the way you can visit Manakamana temple by cable car. It takes approx. 3 hours to complete the sightseeing of the temple.

About Manakamana Temple: Takes 10 minutes cable car ride from a station called Kurintar takes you to the temple of Goddess Manakamana who is believed to have the power to fulfill wishes. Kurintar is 104 km away from Kathmandu on the way to Pokhara.

Lunch: After sightseeing you can have your lunch at the Manakamana restaurant.

Continue Journey: Continue drive to Kathmandu and check-in at hotel upon arrival.

Note: The cable car cost (INR 550 Per Person) isn't included in our given price.

Meals: Breakfast Included

Accommodation: Arts Kathmandu Hotel

Drive Duration: 6-7 hours (with stop)

Distance: Approximately 200 km

DAY 6 KATHMANDU SIGHTSEEING 1,400m

10:00 AM: After breakfast visit for a full-day sightseeing of:

  • Pashupatinath Temple: Largest and holiest shrine for Hindus, situated on bank of Bagmati river
  • Jal Narayan (Budhanilkantha): Temple dedicated to Lord Vishnu with sleeping statue on water
  • Kathmandu Durbar Square: UNESCO site with Hanuman Dhoka, Taleju Temple, Kumari House
  • Patan Durbar Square: Another UNESCO site with Krishna Temple, Bhimsen Temple
  • Baglamukhi Temple: Famous temple of Goddess in Patan (Lalitpur)

Optional Activity: Everest Mountain Flight at 06:00 AM, take-off from Kathmandu Airport (This is about an hour long flight to explore an eye-pleasing view of the Mt. Everest along with other Mountains)

Evening: Free for shopping at Thamel or local markets.

Meals: Breakfast Included

Accommodation: Arts Kathmandu Hotel

DAY 7 DRIVE TO LUMBINI 100m

07:00 AM: After breakfast early in the morning start drive towards Lumbini (Bhairahawa).

Long Drive: Lumbini is about 300 KM west from Kathmandu and takes 10 hours of drive to reach.

Arrival: Upon reaching check in at the hotel in Lumbini (Bhairahawa) and take a rest for the next morning visit to Lumbini cultural area.

Note: This is the longest drive of the itinerary. We recommend carrying snacks and water for the journey.

Meals: Breakfast Included

Accommodation: Bodhi Red Sun Hotel (Lumbini)

Drive Duration: 10 hours

Distance: Approximately 300 km

DAY 8 LUMBINI SIGHTSEEING, DRIVE TO GORAKHPUR 100m

08:00 AM: Early morning visit to the Lumbini cultural area - the birthplace of Lord Buddha.

Lumbini Highlights: Visit Maya Devi Temple (exact birthplace), Ashoka Pillar, sacred pond, and various monasteries built by different countries.

After Sightseeing: Once your sightseeing is over, you will proceed your drive towards Gorakhpur. The driving duration could be about 4-5 hours.

Border Crossing: Complete exit formalities at Nepal-India border.

Journey End: Drop at Gorakhpur airport/railway station as per your onward journey.

Meals: Breakfast Included

May the blessings of Lord Buddha and Lord Vishnu be with you always!

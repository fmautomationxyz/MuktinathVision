Nepal Heritage & Muktinath Tour Package - EX Gorakhpur 8 Days

(Gorakhpur/Lumbini/Pokhara/Muktinath/Kathmandu/Janakpur & Darabhanga)

A spiritually enriching expedition where history, mythology, and devotion unite—offering not just a tour, but a transformative pilgrimage of the soul.

Spiritual heritage journey – Gorakhpur to Muktinath

This 8-day pilgrimage begins in Gorakhpur, taking you through Lumbini (birthplace of Buddha), scenic Pokhara, sacred Muktinath, Kathmandu's UNESCO sites, and Janakpur – the cradle of Sita. A well‑planned express route for devotees and culture lovers.

Journey Highlights

  • Muktinath Darshan: Complete puja and holy bath at 108 waterspouts
  • Lumbini: Mayadevi Temple, Ashoka Pillar – birthplace of Buddha
  • Pokhara: Tal Barahi, Devi’s Fall, Gupteshwor Cave, Bindhyabasini Temple
  • Kathmandu: Pashupatinath, Swayambhunath, Patan Durbar Square
  • Janakpur: Ram Janaki Mandir, Vivah Mandap, Dhanus Sagar
  • Manakamana: Optional cable car to the wishing goddess

Tour Facts

  • Duration: 7 Nights / 8 Days
  • Start/End: Gorakhpur / Darabhanga
  • Maximum Altitude: 3,800m (Muktinath)
  • Best Season: March-May & September-November
  • Group Size: 2-10 Persons
  • Transport: Private A/C vehicle + Jeep to Muktinath

Detailed Itinerary

DAY 1GORAKHPUR ARRIVAL → LUMBINI

Your Arrival Time (?): Our office representative or driver will pick you up from the Gorakhpur airport/railway stations and transfer to Lumbini. Upon reaching Lumbini, check-in at the hotel and rest for your tomorrow's drive to Pokhara.

Meals: Dinner

Accommodation: Hotel in Lumbini (3 Star)

DAY 2LUMBINI SIGHTSEEING → POKHARA

08:00 AM: After your breakfast, visit the Lumbini cultural area. Once your sightseeing is over, start driving towards Pokhara (Approx. 7-8 hrs driving). After reaching Pokhara, check in at the hotel and take a rest.

Meals: Breakfast, Dinner

Accommodation: Hotel in Pokhara (3 Star)

DAY 3DRIVE TO MUKTINATH, VISIT TEMPLE

07:30 AM: Today early in the morning you will start your drive to Muktinath temple, which is about 180 km and takes 6-7 hours from Pokhara. Once you reach Muktinath, complete Muktinath Temple Darshan, Puja and Holy bath and drive down to Jomsom to stay the night.

Meals: Breakfast, Dinner

Accommodation: Hotel in Jomsom (3 Star)

DAY 4DRIVE TO POKHARA, SIGHTSEEING

06:30 AM: After having your breakfast at the hotel today, you will drive to Pokhara, which is about 160 KM and takes 5-6 hours to drive. 01:00 PM: After you reach Pokhara, check in at your hotel, refresh yourself, and get ready for your sightseeing tour. Your tour begins with a visit to Tal Barahi, Devi’s Fall, Gupteshwor Mahadev Cave and Bindhyabasini Temple.

Meals: Breakfast, Dinner

Accommodation: Hotel in Pokhara (3 Star)

DAY 5DRIVE TO KATHMANDU, MANAKAMANA TEMPLE

08:00 AM: After your breakfast, proceed to Kathmandu. On the way you can visit Manakamana temple by cable car. It takes approx. 3 hours. After sightseeing you can have your lunch at the Manakamana restaurant, and continue drive to Kathmandu. Note: Manakamana cable car tickets (INR 670 Per Person) aren't included.

Meals: Breakfast, Dinner

Accommodation: Hotel in Kathmandu (3 Star)

DAY 6FULL-DAY KATHMANDU SIGHTSEEING

10:00 AM: After your breakfast visit for a full-day sightseeing of Pashupatinath Temple, Guhyeshwari Temple, Jal Narayan Temple (Budhanilkantha), Swayambhunath Stupa and Patan Durbar Square. All these are UNESCO listed world heritage sites. Optional: Everest Mountain Flight at 06:00 AM (INR 9,500 per person).

Meals: Breakfast, Dinner

Accommodation: Hotel in Kathmandu (3 Star)

DAY 7DRIVE TO JANAKPUR DHAM

07:00 AM: After breakfast early in the morning start drive towards Janakpur. Janakpur is about 350 KM west from Kathmandu and takes 10 hours to reach. Upon reaching check in at the hotel in Janakpur and take a rest.

Meals: Breakfast, Dinner

Accommodation: Hotel in Janakpur (3 Star)

DAY 8JANAKPUR SIGHTSEEING → DARABHANGA

08:00 AM: Early morning Visit Ram Janaki Mandir, Ram Sita Vivaha Mandap, Dhanus Sagar, and Ganga Sagar. Once your sightseeing is over, you will proceed your drive towards Darabhanga (4-5 hours). Tour ends.

Meals: Breakfast

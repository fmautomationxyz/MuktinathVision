Spiritual Journey to Muktinath

Embark on a transformative 6-day pilgrimage to the sacred Muktinath Temple, one of the most revered spiritual sites in the Himalayas. This meticulously designed package combines comfortable drives, scenic flights, and spiritual experiences to create a seamless journey to the "Place of Liberation" where Lord Vishnu grants salvation to devotees.

Journey Highlights

  • Scenic Mountain Flight: Fly over the Himalayas to Jomsom with breathtaking views
  • Sacred Temple Visit: Complete puja and holy bath at Muktinath's 108 waterspouts
  • Cable Car Experience: Optional visit to Manakamana Temple via cable car
  • Cultural Exploration: Sightseeing in Pokhara and Kathmandu's UNESCO sites
  • Comfortable Travel: Private AC vehicles and domestic flights throughout
  • Spiritual Liberation: Experience the divine energy of this sacred pilgrimage site

Trip Facts

  • Duration: 5 Nights 6 Days
  • Starting Point: Kathmandu, Nepal
  • Transportation: Flight + Private Vehicle
  • Maximum Altitude: 3,710m (Muktinath Temple)
  • Difficulty: Easy to Moderate
  • Best Season: Year-round (Spring & Autumn ideal)
  • Temple Significance: One of the 108 Divya Desams of Lord Vishnu

Why Visit Muktinath?

Muktinath Temple holds immense significance for both Hindus and Buddhists. Hindus believe that visiting this temple brings salvation (Moksha), while Buddhists revere it as a place where Guru Rinpoche meditated. The temple's 108 waterspouts and eternal flame burning under water make it one of the world's most unique spiritual sites.

Detailed Itinerary

DAY 1 ARRIVAL IN KATHMANDU 1,400m

Welcome to Nepal: Upon arrival at Tribhuvan International Airport, our representative will warmly welcome you with traditional greetings. You'll be transferred to your hotel in a comfortable private vehicle. Complete check-in formalities and rest after your journey. If you arrive early and have free time, you can explore nearby areas or add optional sightseeing as per your interest. Our team will brief you about the upcoming spiritual journey.

Note: Early arrivals can add optional Kathmandu sightseeing

Meals: None Included (on arrival day)

Accommodation: 3 Star Hotel in Kathmandu

DAY 2 KATHMANDU TO POKHARA VIA MANAKAMANA 822m

Scenic Drive with Spiritual Stop: After breakfast, begin your scenic drive to Pokhara (approximately 6-7 hours). En route, you have the option to visit the sacred Manakamana Temple via cable car. This temple is dedicated to Goddess Bhagwati and is believed to grant wishes. The cable car ride offers stunning views of the Trishuli River and surrounding hills. Continue your journey to Pokhara, arriving at your hotel in the late afternoon. Check in and relax, or explore Pokhara's famous Lakeside area in the evening.

Optional Activity: Manakamana Cable Car (INR 670 per person round trip)

Meals: Breakfast Included

Accommodation: 3 Star Hotel in Pokhara

Drive Duration: 6-7 hours

Distance: 200 km

DAY 3 FLY TO JOMSOM & DRIVE TO MUKTINATH 3,710m

Sacred Pilgrimage Day: Early morning transfer to Pokhara airport for your scenic flight to Jomsom (approximately 20 minutes). Enjoy breathtaking views of Annapurna and Dhaulagiri ranges during the flight. Have breakfast at a hotel in Jomsom. Then drive by private jeep to Muktinath Temple (approximately 1 hour). Spend the full day performing puja rituals and taking the holy bath under 108 waterspouts. Complete your spiritual activities by 4:00 PM, then drive back to Jomsom for overnight stay.

Important Notes:

  • 30-minute walk uphill from jeep parking to temple
  • Pony ride available at INR 800 round trip (optional)
  • Carry warm clothing as temperatures can drop

Meals: Breakfast Included

Accommodation: Basic Hotel in Jomsom

Flight Duration: 20 minutes

Drive Duration: 1 hour each way

DAY 4 FLY TO POKHARA & SIGHTSEEING

Return to Pokhara with Exploration: Early morning flight back to Pokhara (weather permitting). Transfer to your hotel for check-in and breakfast. Begin half-day sightseeing of Pokhara's major attractions: Visit Tal Barahi Temple situated on an island in Phewa Lake (boat ride included), witness the spectacular Davis Falls where water disappears underground, explore the mystical Gupteshwor Mahadev Cave with its natural Shiva Linga, and seek blessings at Bindhyabasini Temple. Evening free to relax by the lake or explore Pokhara's markets.

Weather Note: Flights from Jomsom are weather-dependent. Alternative 4WD jeep will be arranged if flights cancelled (additional cost may apply for smaller groups)

Meals: Breakfast Included

Accommodation: 3 Star Hotel in Pokhara

Sightseeing: Half-day Pokhara exploration

DAY 5 FLY TO KATHMANDU & SIGHTSEEING

Cultural Exploration in Kathmandu: After breakfast, transfer to Pokhara airport for your flight to Kathmandu (approximately 30 minutes). Upon arrival, transfer to your hotel. At 11:00 AM, begin comprehensive Kathmandu sightseeing: Visit the sacred Pashupatinath Temple (UNESCO site), see the ancient Jal Narayan Temple with its beautiful stone carvings, explore the majestic Swayambhunath Stupa (Monkey Temple) offering panoramic city views, visit Guhyeshwari Temple, and admire the architectural marvels of Patan Durbar Square. Evening free for shopping and personal exploration in Thamel.

Meals: Breakfast Included

Accommodation: 3 Star Hotel in Kathmandu

Flight Duration: 30 minutes

Sightseeing: Full day Kathmandu cultural tour

DAY 6 DEPARTURE FROM KATHMANDU

Journey Completion: Enjoy a leisurely breakfast at your hotel. You'll have free time until your departure flight. Our transportation service will pick you up from the hotel 3 hours before your flight time and transfer you to Tribhuvan International Airport. We'll assist with check-in procedures for your onward journey. Depart with blessed memories of your spiritual journey to Muktinath and the beautiful experiences of Nepal.

Optional Extension: If you wish to extend your stay in Nepal, we can arrange additional tours to Chitwan National Park, Lumbini (Birthplace of Buddha), or Everest region.

Optional Activity: Everest Mountain Flight at 6:30 AM - 50-minute aerial tour of Himalayas including Mt. Everest (INR 9,500 per person with window seat)

Meals: Breakfast Included

May Lord Vishnu's blessings of Mukti (liberation) be with you always!
